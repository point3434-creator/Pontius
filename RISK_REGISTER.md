# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Warning sign | Mitigation / stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Metrics fail to predict full-game strength | Exact improvements reverse in every realistic league | Freeze hidden suites; build independent responders; stop architecture work if measurement is untrustworthy |
| R2 | Incorrect rules or regret math | Failure to reproduce small known games | Slow reference, property tests, differential implementations; correctness blocks optimization |
| R3 | Multiplayer CFR gives misleading apparent convergence | Self-play improves while unilateral deviations grow | Report exact NashConv in tractable games; compare current, average, and snapshot policies |
| R4 | Neural leaves have low MSE but damage root play | Root harm worsens on insertion | Root-aware gates; retain blueprint continuation; stop scaling the model until targets are fixed |
| R5 | Adaptive search neglects rare adversarial branches | Restricted responder steers into cold lines | Minimum coverage and worst-case vulnerability budgets |
| R6 | Learned scheduler overfits its oracle | Beats heuristic only on training games | Hidden games, conservative uncertainty bonus, permanent heuristic fallback |
| R7 | Premature kernel optimization | Fast synthetic benchmark with no end-to-end gain | Optimize only captured representative traces |
| R8 | Host/GPU memory exhaustion | Peak use leaves no transient headroom | Target <=48 GB host and <=13 GB GPU steady state; report bytes per structure and enforce quotas |
| R9 | Single-developer scope overload | Multiple incomplete subsystems | One active checkpoint, three immediate tasks, explicit kill criteria |
| R10 | Agreement bias hides flaws | Decisions contain no opposing evidence | Mandatory dissent format and falsifying experiment for consequential choices |
| R11 | Inaccessible Pluribus prevents headline comparison | Internal league becomes sole evidence | Maintain a Pluribus-style control, independent responders, and precise limited claims |
| R12 | Shallow resolving damages a strong blueprint even with exact leaves | Exact-control NashConv exceeds blueprint NashConv | Require paired exact-control gate, variant-neutral anchoring, and an explicit blueprint/no-op fallback before neural scaling |
| R13 | Anchor and no-op thresholds overfit independent errors in a tiny game | Selected coefficient reverses under correlated errors or a new player count | Freeze selection rules before held-out games; require reach-weighted structured-error and multiplayer gates |
| R14 | Joint-reach weighting hides rare but strategically pivotal value errors | Equal on-policy root error causes greater harm when localized in cold branches | Track per-player counterfactual error and local uncertainty; enforce minimum coverage; test adversarial branch targeting |
| R15 | Low leaf risk is mistaken for positive value of search | Near-exact leaves still produce a worse candidate in some depths or blueprint regimes | Require an independent resolver-benefit lower bound; risk may veto but never authorize search by itself |
| R16 | Local resolver improvement fails when composed with unsolved continuation play | Local NashConv falls while full-game NashConv rises, even with exact continuation values | Evaluate a complete continual resolver; track policy displacement; require full-game or safe-frontier evidence before freezing a benefit gate |
| R17 | A common Bayesian posterior is mistaken for a safe replacement frontier | Posterior-correct continual resolves improve locally but open profitable earlier or off-path deviations | Reproduce two-player opponent-CF-value gadget guarantees; treat multiplayer constraints as measured relaxations, not transferred theorems |
| R18 | A theorem-correct gadget is mistaken for an efficient resolver | Finite CFR residual permits harm, or exact certification rejects nearly every candidate and costs more than the gain | Measure one-sided residual and certificate latency; keep strict no-op; compare against an exact constrained/max-margin oracle before optimizing kernels |
| R19 | Constraint generation hides exponential work inside pricing or separation | Small LPs look fast while responder traversals or active scenarios dominate latency | Price with dynamic best response; report active rows/columns and phase timing; require held-out quality/ms before kernel work |
| R20 | Similar-looking ranges authorize an invalid strategy-cache hit | A rare blocker hand has a different conditional range or best response despite high root overlap | Deploy cached strategies only on exact joint-range provenance; treat structural/approximate matches as topology reuse or warm starts; recertify under the current range |
| R21 | Two-player safety language is transferred to multiplayer constraints | Unilateral metrics look acceptable while a coalition or correlated field response causes large loss | Call multiplayer output constraint-screened, not safe; report unilateral NashConv plus explicit coalition/team best-response threat models before deployment claims |
| R22 | A scheduler feature is accidentally equivalent to its exact target in a shallow game | Regret has near-perfect rank correlation only where every player acts once | Prove and test small-game identities; require transfer to sequential multi-action trees before fitting or freezing a scheduler |
| R23 | Scheduler evidence optimizes free features or total headroom instead of quality per millisecond | High opportunity correlation collapses after dividing by future work, or an allocator uses a feature before paying its probe cost | Make gain per deterministic work the primary target, replicate with measured milliseconds, enforce a minimum paid checkpoint in allocation controls, and reject rules that cannot beat the matched fixed checkpoint |
| R24 | Development cross-validation is mistaken for unseen transfer after the feature family was designed on the same development trace | Internal folds pass but a fixed rule reverses on new boards | Label internal folds as development only; commit one selected rule before constructing validation; reject without retuning if any frozen validation gate fails |
| R25 | A degenerate exact teacher cycles or violates floating-point simplex tolerances | Pivot count explodes or primal/dual verification aborts on a new normal form | Use a bounded packing fast path plus two-phase fallback; record backend and pivots; require original-matrix duality and behavioral NashConv verification for every teacher |
| R26 | A small exact heads-up river scheduling win is mistaken for a six-player or wider-tree result | The frozen rule loses its rate edge after adding bet sizes, deeper action sequences, belief shifts, inference, or concurrent runtime contention | Keep the rule as a narrow control; require held-out richer-action and multiplayer gates with all feature, recertification, queueing, and inference costs before any production or neural-scheduler claim |
| R27 | Exact-source cache reuse looks nearly free because source construction is treated as sunk | Warm or recertified reuse wins only when a 100+ ms exact teacher is excluded, then reverses for finite policies or low reuse counts | Test finite source checkpoints, record source residual and construction cost, report break-even reuse counts, and require an exact incremental-recertification control before cache promotion |
| R28 | Sparse explicit range deltas overstate cache speed under real Bayesian updates | Two-deal perturbations are cheap, but conditioning on an observed action reweights most legal joint deals and invalidates most aggregates | Cross the exact dependency control with factorized player-likelihood and low-rank dense updates; report invalidation cone and no-sharing cost; keep the sparse kernel as an oracle if the advantage collapses |
| R29 | Exact-label no-op filtering makes unsafe adaptive search look deployable | Mask/no-op oracle passes while raw shallow solves have large aggregate harm or a causal rule cannot identify rejection cases | Report raw harm beside oracle-filtered quality; keep future labels out of features; charge any recertifier; require group-separated causal transfer before native or neural work |
| R30 | Independent atomic certificates are composed as if safety were additive | Each atom passes against the blueprint but their union changes response selectors or breaches a cap | Treat the blueprint as immutable; prohibit chaining by default; exactly recertify every proposed union and record same-seat/cross-seat interactions |
| R31 | A retrospective 15-second per-street ledger is mistaken for a live rule | The best scale or direction is selected after an off-clock sweep, then described as available online | Separate identification from deployment; authorize only precommitted one-attempt rules on fresh targets; reserve emission time and reject late results |
| R32 | Bitwise GPU equality rejects numerically identical evidence or encourages post-hoc tolerance changes | A valid parallel reduction changes a digest, or a ceiling is introduced only after failure | Use preregistered numerical identity for Float64 recomputation; retain exact digests for provenance; fail any unplanned tolerance relaxation |
| R33 | Resident GPU pools leave insufficient transient or driver headroom | Persistent bytes fit but cached scratch or a second context drives physical free memory below the frozen floor | Measure pool total and physical free memory separately; trim scratch before readiness; stop widening when either preregistered ceiling fails |
| R34 | Update magnitude is mistaken for certified opportunity | Regret mass predicts the soft update but is uncorrelated or negatively correlated with the best admissible direction | Evaluate opportunity features against exact certified value, not policy motion; keep the immutable envelope and run fresh selector tests before authorization |
| R35 | Documentation drift changes the apparent active experiment or runtime contract | Front doors name an old ADR, let the 15-second street authority age out of view, preserve an obsolete “next” step, or silently forget a revoked authority | Require the latest ADR to carry an explicit complete front-door snapshot naming research head, process head, runtime-contract head, cumulative revocations, active next, and blockers; validate every historical snapshot and the contiguous filename/title ADR chain; reject partial or omitted metadata; keep contract and revocations permanently visible; never classify state from title/status substrings |
| R36 | Coincidental equality between distinct semantic quantities lets a wrong formula pass every gate | A stack, pot, or other convenient field is reused as the payoff span and remains numerically equal at one size | Derive guards and quality normalization only through shared layout/game helpers; keep a two-size mutation control and an exact AST inventory of frozen legacy expressions; block every new occurrence |
| R37 | An incomplete or ill-conditioned one-seat master is mistaken for a safe global optimizer | A cap-feasible restricted-master point is emitted before full epigraph separation, the timeout gap is reported as `L - U`, or near-parallel exact cuts are dropped at the simplex tolerance | Separate every opponent epigraph, keep an independently certified feasible incumbent, report `U - L`, deduplicate only exact response tapes, retain conditioning diagnostics, and require an independently enumerated finite teacher before h4/h32 scaling |
| R38 | Structured fixed-panel breadth is mistaken for an IID deployment success rate | “10 of 12” Latin breadth or “6 of 6” post-call shadow acceptance is quoted without the retained-board/source and action-history design | Call each result fixed-panel evidence only; keep every abstention and sub-material acceptance visible; require new posterior/action regimes with unchanged gates before estimating an operational rate |
| R39 | A facet-closed LP point is mistaken for an exact-cap-feasible global incumbent | No new response signature remains, but the master accepts a `1e-10`–`1e-9` cap-boundary residual that exceeds the certificate's `2e-11` allowance, leaving `U - L` open | Keep exact cap feasibility and `U - L` separate from facet closure; retain the blueprint/retreat fallback; diagnose scaling or high-accuracy primal refinement without loosening the certificate; require an independently certified feasible incumbent for global closure |
| R40 | An artifact digest names an in-memory serialization rather than the bytes that reached disk | A recorded hash matches canonical text but not `Path.read_bytes()` after platform newline translation or another write-time transformation | Write canonical bytes directly, atomically replace, reread, and hash the persisted bytes; test the helper under newline-sensitive payloads; never retrofit or silently rewrite a sealed artifact |
| R41 | A frozen campaign wall limit is checked only after all bounded work has completed | A runner crosses its global deadline, continues opening timing or strategy labels, and rejects only during final gate assembly | Carry one monotonic deadline through the shared deadline module; checkpoint before each target or arm; stop before starting a bounded unit when the remaining campaign allowance is insufficient; keep the campaign ceiling separate from the one shared 15,000 ms per-street live ledger, which never resets per action |
| R42 | A speculative current-node row cache aliases a different policy, belief, prefix, response tape, or implementation | A cache hit is exact at its stored source point but its coefficients or response facet disagree under the current continuation | Require literal persisted-byte identity plus a full policy, belief, game provenance, continuation topology, action schema, acting/payoff role, fixed-response-tape, primitive, and numerical-contract digest; validate the row at the source probabilities; treat every miss or mismatch as immutable-blueprint fallback |
| R43 | A semantically required cache input remains caller supplied after the persisted bytes pass | A finite acting best-response scalar changes the assembled gain while the cache still reports an exact hit, or cache-backed rows are paired with a different valid caller blueprint | Bind every assembly input under one externally trusted persisted-byte hash; return the bundle atomically; remove the public scalar assembler and duplicate caller inputs; require the caller blueprint digest to match the cache identity; reject older schemas instead of migrating them implicitly |
| R44 | Pass-looking canonical evidence is written after the last bounded campaign unit or a publication race overwrites prior evidence | Hashing, gate assembly, serialization, write, promotion, canonical readback, sealing, or unlocking crosses the campaign wall; staged bytes change; or a target appears between an existence check and publication | Preregister bounded data and seal phases; exclusively stage and publish without clobbering; keep a durable publishing lock until canonical data and completion seal reread and pass postchecks; preserve lock-marked failure diagnostics; consume only through the verified loader |
| R45 | A feasible minimization primal objective is labeled as a lower bound | A recorded `U - L` becomes slightly negative even though solver residual gates pass | Derive `L` only from an outward-rounded dual/Lagrangian certificate with explicit bounded-variable residual correction; report raw primal/dual values separately; retain matrices and multipliers needed for replay |
| R46 | A local affine row erases payoff role or silently omits upstream own reach | Crossed payoff/source values pass the source intercept, or a nonroot repeated actor disagrees with exact endpoint evaluation | Carry acting and payoff roles in a typed result; require an explicit match; fail closed outside the root until upstream own realization reach is represented and independently differenced |
| R47 | One numerical tolerance controls distinct semantic gates, or generic numeric fields permit accidental cross-wiring | Changing a relative reversal knob also changes absolute closure, changing selector margin also changes intercept identity, or either GPU residual allowance is routed to the other gate | Use exact config schemas and nominally distinct types for every semantic allowance; mutation-test one field at a time; forbid legacy alias keys in successors |
| R48 | A GPU raw-pointer fold trusts caller-supplied device indices separately from host topology | Rolled or out-of-range device indices change results or address memory beyond the hand axis | Make the validated host Int32 mapping the sole authority; check seat, shape, contiguity, and range before launch; upload the device copy internally; keep duplicate device-index parameters out of successor APIs |
| R49 | Evidence plumbing trusts a shallow mutable parse or an incomplete pass-looking artifact | Config bytes change between hashing and parsing, nested data mutates after validation, duplicate keys or NaN select a parser-dependent value, or `{passed: true}` substitutes for a complete schema | Read one bounded regular-file descriptor snapshot; reject duplicate/nonfinite JSON; require a consumer-complete schema; hash and parse the same bytes; deep-freeze the result; keep historical shallow loaders out of successor runners |
| R50 | A tolerated negative reach silently carries payoff numerator mass or borrows a unit floor | Near-zero reach receives the same allowance as unit-scale reach, or clamping reach to zero leaves numerator in the hand aggregate | Give absolute and relative reach errors distinct types; scale relative allowance only by positive reach; derive the numerator bound from the game payoff span; reject nonfinite values; discard numerator and reach together; differential-test the complete transitive GPU path |
| R51 | Chip-valued scales relax dimensionless master feasibility or a nonfinite solver output passes comparisons | A large payoff cap admits probabilities outside `[0,1]`, NaN evades residual gates, or one generic tolerance changes unrelated KKT conditions | Validate every row and solver vector as finite; keep probability equality/bound tolerances independent of chip-valued inequality, epigraph, complementarity and objective-gap tolerances; mutation-test large-cap and NaN cases |
| R52 | A numerically conserving betting engine implements the wrong no-limit semantic rule | A single short all-in reopens a prior caller, cumulative short all-ins fail to reopen seat-specifically, folded-only thresholds award repeated odd chips, or an uncalled overbet remains in a pot | Name the Poker TDA full-bet profile; track each seat's last faced wager and the largest full increment separately; return unmatched chips; distinguish contribution layers from actual pots; retain published-example, randomized, and exhaustive independent-oracle tests |
| R53 | Street timing either charges opponent idle or hides agent work between timers | A late-position seat loses budget while doing nothing, useful background work is described as free, initialization/legality/event/fallback/transition work occurs outside the ledger, or a reset erases the prior street's audit record | Accumulate explicit monotonic charged intervals in the one-seat controller; charge live factory construction, event processing, foreground/background work, selection, fallback, transition, and emission; pause only opponent/transport idle; archive each closing snapshot before exact reset and freeze the terminal street; reject nested intervals and caller-directed resets |
| R54 | The exact integer-chip reference is mistaken for a scalable action abstraction or complete bot | A full-stack integer raise range is enumerated in a solver, an explicit-deal replay is described as a trained full-game agent, or a passive smoke source is credited with strategy quality | Keep exact legality and candidate generation separate; preserve the ADR-0290 replay as a reference oracle; require a separately preregistered and passed action-abstraction gate plus a trained blueprint before resolver integration; compare every abstraction against the exact kernel |
| R55 | Opponent or future cards leak from an explicit replay deal into a one-seat decision | A blueprint key contains a full deal/runout, a preflop view changes when only opponent/future cards change, or single-opponent domains differ from 1,225/1,081/1,035/990 | Separate `SixSeatHoldemDeal` oracle state from `OneSeatCardState`; key only the controlled pair and currently revealed board; require exact transition, distinctness, blindness, deterministic random-deal, and combo-domain tests; reject any future reveal or overlap |
| R56 | An immutable reference action source is stale, mutable, illegal, or mistaken for a credible blueprint | Duplicate or aliased entries change after hashing, a stale raise bypasses current bounds, missing keys invent raise sizes, or passive fixture actions are called strategy evidence | Deep-freeze semantic keys/actions, bind canonical bytes to a digest, reject duplicate keys, revalidate every hit against `LegalBettingDecision`, use only check/call/fold as the missing-key total rule, and keep the untrained/passive claims boundary explicit |
| R57 | A scripted complete-hand replay self-certifies its own cards, pots, or time coverage | Production side-pot assembly is reused as the only oracle, a replay operation has no matching charged interval, post-terminal evaluator time is called decision time, or one explicit runout is generalized to live dealing | Retain frozen action-order and showdown fixtures, a separate chip-depth pot/payout oracle, manually fixed rank ordering, interval-to-ledger bijection checks, adversarial event mutations, and explicit-chance scope; require separate shuffle/transport/live-hand gates |
| R58 | Independent opponent ranges or compact factor storage are mistaken for a collision-aware solved joint belief | Separately normalized unaries are reported as joint marginals, Cartesian assignments are called compatible support, a folded seat's blockers disappear, or a compact five-axis object is credited with full-width contraction accuracy or speed | Keep all five axes and the hard disjointness factor, distinguish Cartesian and compatible counts, retain folded axes, validate reduced supports/partitions/marginals against an independent exact rational oracle, bind backend bytes and exact update provenance, and make no normalized full-width marginal or contraction claim until separately gated |
| R59 | A low-powered sizing panel invites post-outcome action tuning or makes a quality gate vacuous | Most contexts give identical full and narrow values, one missing observed size explains the only gap, a structurally diverse untouched panel still has too few informative contexts, or a successor adds an observed size and reuses the same panel as confirmation | Label opened panels development-only; freeze generators and seeds before construction; keep structural diversity distinct from sizing power; reject without retuning when power fails; before v3, preregister a candidate-blind full-versus-narrow power diagnostic or richer reduced game |
| R60 | Caller-owned evidence stopping permits post-target labels to open after an unattributed failure | An assertion says only `11 != 12`, a follow-up guesses the wrong batch, a scratch loop evaluates contexts after the twelfth qualifier, or a manually assembled result claims a terminal state inconsistent with its observations | Put batch identity, pool-bound contiguous observations, stop reason, and target/exhaustion/first-ambiguity invariants in the value-owning runner and result; return immediately at target or ambiguity; prohibit scratch value loops; record and quarantine every post-stop opening |
| R61 | A richer evaluation game is mistaken for production range width, action quality, or runtime cost | Four private types improve qualification yield, deterministic LP dimensions or pivots are described as milliseconds, or a conditioned development panel is called representative | Change one game axis at a time; keep candidate values absent; gate replicated yield and exact LP work separately; report machine wall time only as a local diagnostic; require fresh candidate and confirmation preregistrations before any integration claim |
| R62 | A generic LP residual hides constraints with incompatible units behind one numerical maximum | Probability-simplex and chip-valued envelope rows share a `max_constraint_violation` field, an equal numerical ceiling is treated as semantic equivalence, or a mixed-coordinate diagnostic is reported as a physical bound | Reconstruct and expose dimensionless probability feasibility and chip-valued envelope feasibility separately; use distinct nominal allowances; label the legacy mixed maximum diagnostic-only and never scale one allowance through the other |
| R63 | A state-adaptive sizing rule silently drops a prior action or tunes itself to opened values | The two-pot origin changes despite being distinct, fallback selection consults cards/ranges/value, provenance omits the chosen branch, or a collision repair widens beyond the frozen ceiling | Permit the three-halves fallback only when clipped two-pot equals a mandatory legal anchor; prove v2 action-set inclusion exhaustively; bind the chosen exact fraction and collision decision; freeze source and seeds before panels; retain nine-action fail-closed width |
| R64 | A value-free structural record becomes a different game during qualification, or candidate information enters the power screen | Cards, chips, exact weights, order, or payoff span change in conversion; the qualifier imports v3; representative values open during pool classification; or observations continue after target or ambiguity | Use an explicit one-to-one semantic adapter with field/digest equality; keep structural and value-owning modules separate; statically forbid candidate imports; bind the sealed pool and contiguous prefix; stop inside the runner immediately at 24 qualifiers, first ambiguity, or exhaustion; keep representative and v3 values unopened until the qualified panel is committed |
