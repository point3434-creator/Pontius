# ADR-0292: Reject action abstraction v1 before complete-hand integration

- Status: accepted mixed engineering result; exact lattice mechanics pass, candidate sizing quality fails, and v1 is rejected before replay integration
- Date: 2026-08-23
- Follows: ADR-0291
- Front-Door-Kind: controller-v1
- Front-Door-Research: ADR-0280
- Front-Door-Process: ADR-0292
- Front-Door-Contract: ADR-0282
- Front-Door-Revoked: ADR-0281
- Front-Door-Active-Next: Preregister a non-rescuing development/confirmation successor for action abstraction; keep v1 parked and unintegrated, and keep blueprint training, convex-master integration, resolving, and strategy labels closed
- Front-Door-Blockers: v1 has only one nondegenerate frozen context and recovers none of its full-over-narrow gain; no successor mechanism, untouched confirmation panel, anti-overfit selection rule, or charged replay gate is frozen

## Verdict

Reject ADR-0291's candidate sizing lattice before complete-hand integration.
Its exact legal-action and projection machinery passes the bounded correctness
controls, but its preregistered reduced sizing-quality gate does not. The
failure is clean, independently reproduced, and may not be rescued by changing
the fraction list, contexts, thresholds, rounding, or interpolation after the
outcome. ADR-0290's exact integer betting, full-width symbolic belief, rational
reference policy, and passive complete-hand loop remain the active C5
foundation.

## Decision

Keep the immutable v1 lattice, exact rational projector, compact reduced river
LP, and bounded normal-form teacher as parked research controls. They are not
exported as the package decision boundary and are not connected to the
complete-hand replay, convex master, blueprint source, or resolver.

Treat this invocation as development evidence only. A successor must be
preregistered as a new hypothesis with a visible development/confirmation
split: it may use this opened panel to motivate a mechanism, but the final
algorithm and selection rule must be frozen before any untouched confirmation
panel is constructed or opened. It must compare against full integer, v1, and
minimum/all-in controls in identical chip units and retain the exact legality,
provenance, width, off-tree, and 15-second charged-work contracts. No isolated
addition of the observed missing three-chip size is authorized.

## Exact-state result

The source retains every legal non-raise literally; adds exact minimum,
maximum-contestable, own-all-in, and four rational pot-fraction origins; records
low/high clipping; deduplicates only after integer projection; and retains at
most seven raises and nine total actions. The projector uses a distinct exact
rational weight type, maps every off-tree raise to adjacent retained raises,
sums exactly to one, and preserves exact raise-to expectation without changing
the public betting state.

The exhaustive control reaches the frozen 45,456 semantic states and 60,732
transitions, including 22,920 active decisions. Every retained action applies
through the exact betting kernel, and every exact legal integer action has a
valid one- or two-atom projection. Deterministic 100-big-blind, short-all-in,
cumulative reopening, unequal-stack contestable/all-in, clipped/deduplicated,
stale-context, illegal-action, mutable-alias, numerical-boolean, and trillion-
chip interval controls pass. A pre-result inspection caught and repaired lower
neighbor selection that had attempted to order semantic action records rather
than their exact raise-to coordinates; the regression test now exercises the
off-tree path directly.

These are reusable correctness results for a candidate generator interface.
They do not validate v1's bet sizes strategically.

Every compact LP remains within `9.993e-16` probability-simplex residual and
`1.453e-13` chip-objective reconstruction error. Monotonic ordering holds
within `1e-9`. The four bounded two-hand/two-bet complete normal forms agree
with the compact LP within `4.441e-15` chips. Width passes: v1 has five to seven
total actions and, on stack-20 contexts, strictly fewer than half the full
actions. Maximum normalized full-v1 loss is `0.0244201%`; its four-context mean
is `0.00610501%`, both below their ceilings.

Two mandatory gates nevertheless fail:

- only one context, not at least two, has `full - min/all-in` above
  `1e-6 * payoff_span`; and
- in that sole positive-gap context v1 equals the min/all-in control within
  numerical error, so aggregate recovered gain is effectively `0%`, not at
  least `80%`.

Small absolute loss does not override either conjunctive gate.

## Adversarial verification

The informative full LP uses only bets 3 and 12. An independent complete
normal-form enumeration with three opener hands, three responder hands, two
bets, 27 opener pure plans, and 64 responder pure plans gives
`0.646153846154` chips for `(3, 12)` and `0.637362637363` for `(2, 12)`, matching
the compact LP within `3e-13` chips. Thus the result is not caused by an unused
integer action, payoff-span normalization, per-iteration/per-solve confusion,
or a chip/NashConv unit cross-wire.

The complete-hand trace was deliberately not connected after this kill
criterion fired. Consequently there is no claim about 24 charged abstraction
builds, 20 charged opponent projections, replay invariance, full-game quality,
or live latency for v1.

## Evidence classification

- **Known:** the exact betting kernel and ADR-0290 reference loop remain the
  fallback authority.
- **Reproduced:** v1 legality/projection invariants over the frozen exhaustive
  state graph and reduced LP/normal-form agreement.
- **Observed:** v1 misses both conjunctive reduced-quality gates above.
- **Rejected:** v1 as the fixed C5 action abstraction and any post-outcome
  rescue on this opened panel.
- **Hypothesis:** a mechanism selected on development contexts and frozen
  before a genuinely untouched confirmation panel can retain similar width
  while recovering material sizing value.

## Dissent

Supporting rejection: the gate was frozen before the values were opened, two
independent game formulations agree, and the failure is directly attributable
to action availability rather than legality or numerical verification.

Opposing evidence: v1's maximum normalized loss is only about `0.0244%`, all
legality controls pass, and three contexts are degenerate enough that no small
lattice can demonstrate gain over the narrow control. Those facts make the
lattice potentially useful as a baseline, but do not satisfy the frozen gate.

Largest unknown: whether the one informative context exposes a general flaw in
fixed pot fractions or merely an unlucky, low-powered development panel.

Cheapest falsifying successor: preregister a generator-selection mechanism on
the now-open development panel, freeze it without seeing new exact deal/range
contexts, then require material gain recovery and bounded full-action loss on
an untouched confirmation panel at the same width.

## Claims boundary

This record establishes a rejected reduced sizing candidate plus passing
spot-checked legality and oracle controls. It does not certify all no-limit
states, choose a production abstraction, authorize action width through the
convex master, train a blueprint, normalize full-width marginals, resolve a
street, establish multiplayer safety, report NashConv, AIVAT, league strength,
optimized latency, or complete C5. No revoked experiment, external publication,
or thesis change is authorized.
