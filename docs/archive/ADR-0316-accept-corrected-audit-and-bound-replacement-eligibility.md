## Bounded solver direction

The next solver question is not whether Pontius can replace thirty years of
general-purpose numerical engineering. It is whether Pontius can reduce
overhead or exploit its actual repeated master structure while keeping HiGHS
as a correctness and fallback authority.

The prospective order is:

1. Freeze a label-free structural census and identical real master-update
   sequence. Confirm rather than assume the per-hand simplex blocks, coupling
   row count, dynamic cut behavior, and which rows/columns/objective entries
   change between rounds and decisions.
2. Attribute current end-to-end time among model construction, Python/native
   marshaling, presolve, basis work, solve, semantic verification, and emission.
3. Test a direct persistent HiGHS dual-simplex model with modify-in-place
   updates and valid warm-basis reuse against the unchanged rebuild path. This
   is the null hypothesis that can end native-solver work cheaply.
4. Only if material complete-ledger headroom remains, test a decomposition or
   low-dimensional-dual proposer specialized to the confirmed product-of-
   simplexes shape. Treat it as untrusted: independently verify primal and
   dual feasibility, gap, units, and consumer semantics, then fall back to
   HiGHS on every rejection.

The later gate must use identical LPs and certificates, separate cold and warm
traces, charge verifier and fallback time, and measure marginal chip-valued
decision quality per additional millisecond inside the 15-second action wall.
It must not compare the 177-base audit's backend-call totals with production
master latency, per-iteration with per-decision time, or systems speed with
strategy quality. Kill the specialized proposer on any undetected certified
violation, on fallback frequency that erases its gain, or when persistent
HiGHS matches its end-to-end quality-per-millisecond frontier.

This section is a direction for the next preregistration, not implementation
authority.

## Claims boundary

This result establishes one deterministic post-outcome correction over one
exact finite artifact. It provides bounded evidence that the recorded HiGHS
configurations passed the frozen exact-micro and reduced-sizing audit and makes
dual simplex eligible for a later prospective adapter evaluation. It is
spot-checking over a frozen corpus, not certification of all Pontius masters or
all numerical conditions. HiGHS DS and IPM remain two modes of one library,
not independent implementations.

It does not establish an adapter, persistent warm-model validity, latency,
15-second feasibility, marginal decision quality per millisecond, solver
superiority, or poker strength. It establishes no action quality, production
range width, earlier-street quality, complete-hand/live-host integration,
blueprint, resolver, NashConv, AIVAT, league strength, coalition safety, or
complete bot. Native simplex remains rejected. V1-v4 remain parked. No revoked
experiment, external publication, consumer migration, or thesis change is
authorized.
