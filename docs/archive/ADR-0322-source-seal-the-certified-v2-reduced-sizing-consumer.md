## Question

solver-trust, rejection, and no-action boundary before any fresh action-width

## Decision

Yes, as a source-only engineering checkpoint. Accept and canonical-LF seal the
new additive `pontius.certified_reduced_sizing_consumer_v2` and its adjacent
manifest. ADR-0320 remains the eligibility authority: All 177 ordered
observations pass with one public HiGHS-DS call each under HiGHS 1.12.0, keeping
the separately preregistered consumer eligible. ADR-0321 remains the exact
consumer contract and caller-owned legal fallback boundary.

The new module is a research evaluator only. It constructs or emits no
`BettingAction`, imports no historical reduced-sizing consumer or v1-v4 owner,
and has no state mutation path. An accepted result is evidence for one
fold/call-only reduced game; a rejected result requires the caller-owned legal
fallback and contains no candidate action.

## Exact legal binding

The request carries an immutable six-seat betting state, the fixed
heads-up-fold/call response-model identity, exact rational joint
probabilities, exact showdown signs, a full-versus-strict-subset scope, and
nominal `KernelRaiseToTotal` records. The current legal decision is always
rederived from the immutable state; no caller-supplied decision can go stale.

Preflight accepts only two live seats on the river at an unopened
check-or-raise decision. It requires a legal raise interval, a positive even
pot, and equality of the separately retained own maximum and maximum
contestable totals. Every supplied amount must be ordered, kernel legal, and
the exact minimum/maximum anchors must remain present. Complete scope is
checked against every integer in the interval; a restricted tuple cannot
claim it, and a complete tuple cannot claim restricted scope.

`KernelRaiseToTotal` and `ReducedBetIncrement` are different nominal types.
The consumer derives each increment by subtracting
`street_contribution + call_amount`. Its nonzero-contribution control binds
legal totals `(5, 23)` to LP wagers `(2, 20)`. The accepted record retains the
exact request and independently recompiles/rebinds its state, action mapping,
digests, and LP identity in `__post_init__`; mutation of either amount view
rejects.

The semantic request SHA-256 excludes the human context label. Two otherwise
identical complete-universe controls with different labels produce identical
request and legal-set identities. The response model, state, decision,
probabilities, showdown signs, scope, raise totals, increments, and protocol
remain inside the semantic boundary.

## Consequences and next boundary

The source-sealed consumer retires the legal-plumbing portion of the sizing
solver migration without pretending to solve the strategic action-width
question. The strongest next hypothesis is not a fifth ladder revision. It is
a fresh owner that separates three kinds of evidence:

1. exact finite reduced-game regret of a supplied subset against the complete
   legal integer universe;
2. bounded optimal-subset-by-width teachers and own-action block-pricing
   diagnostics, each bound to the exact opponent rows under which it is valid;
   and
3. transfer of one frozen mechanism to a wholly fresh population.

An own-block reduced cost under incumbent response rows is a pricing signal,
not the finite value of admitting the block. Any generation method must
alternate own-block and opponent-response violations until both close. Sized
preparation artifacts may later be tested under ADR-0307, but only exact
provenance hits are creditable and they never enlarge the live 15-second wall.

## Claims boundary

This result establishes a source-sealed, fail-closed software consumer and
unsealed toy/corruption controls for one narrow reduced game. It does not
certify arbitrary inputs, choose an action width, validate any ladder, produce
an exact regret curve, establish column or row closure, model responder raises
or more than one opponent, integrate a live strategy, emit a legal action,
measure preparation utility or complete-decision latency, cover h32/full
ranges or earlier streets, train a blueprint, or establish AIVAT, NashConv,
league, coalition, or poker strength. Systems correctness is not a quality
prior. No revoked experiment, external publication, or thesis change is
authorized.
