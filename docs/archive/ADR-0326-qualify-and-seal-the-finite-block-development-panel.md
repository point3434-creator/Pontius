## Decision

Accept the single ADR-0325 invocation. The frozen pool reaches its sixteenth
material, unambiguous full-over-width-two context at pool index 50 and stops.
Seal the exact 16-context development panel. No context after index 50, no
raise width from three through six, no greedy candidate, and no transfer value
was opened.

This is a qualification result, not an action-width result. It establishes
that the reduced game has enough sizing opportunity to support the already
preregistered bounded teacher. It does not establish which width or mechanism
is good.

The inherited trust chain is unchanged. ADR-0318 binds HiGHS 1.12.0, and
ADR-0319 requires one public HiGHS-DS call per canonical task. All 177 ordered
observations pass under ADR-0320 and keep the certified-v2 consumer eligible.
ADR-0321 retains the caller-owned legal fallback, while ADR-0322 returns
research evidence or rejection with no action. ADR-0323 remains the value-free
finite-block protocol; ADR-0324's unopened remainder stays value-unopened; and
ADR-0325 authorized this qualification exactly once.

## Independent artifact rebinding

Additive source `pontius.fresh_action_width_qualification_result` does not run
the campaign or call a solver. It verifies exact artifact bytes and canonical
JSON, reconstructs every value-free request through the exact betting kernel,
and compares each retained request, public-state, legal-set, LP, consumer
source/protocol, raise-total, and one-call identity with that reconstruction.

It then decodes only canonical finite float-hex values, verifies each lower and
upper endpoint plus signed/nonnegative certificate gap, recomputes
`[L_full-U_subset, U_full-L_subset]`, reclassifies with the context-derived
payoff span and the distinct frozen controls, and reconstructs the complete
qualification-result digest. Finally it rebuilds panel membership from the
sealed pool and reproduces the panel digest. A patched campaign runner and a
patched `scipy.optimize.linprog` both remain unreachable during the maintained
artifact verification test.

The post-outcome result owner can verify and expose only already-retained
evidence. It cannot change a gate, add an observation, or authorize a value.
Its source closure and result protocol are now sealed for downstream teacher
construction.

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** committed artifact bytes, all retained endpoint/identity fields,
  exact prefix and stop state, result and panel digests, and future panel work.
- **Reproduced without solving:** request/legal/LP identities, conservative
  regret arithmetic, classification, result digest, panel membership, and
  panel digest.
- **Observed:** one target-reached 51-context/102-call qualification campaign
  and its `1.4875`-second local elapsed diagnostic.
- **Unopened:** pool contexts 51 through 95, all development values at raise
  widths three through six, every greedy price/choice, and every transfer
  structure/value.
- **Hypothesis:** a small greedy raise width can approximate the exhaustive
  best-subset teacher on this qualified reduced-game panel and later transfer.
- **Rejected:** treating qualification yield, structural diversity, or the
  short offline campaign as action-width quality, 15-second capacity, or poker
  strength.

Supporting acceptance: the exact preregistered target was reached without
ambiguity or rejection, every opened result rebinds independently, and the
result owner cannot rerun the campaign.

Opposing evidence: the panel is deliberately conditioned on material sizing
opportunity, the game has only four private types and one heads-up fold/call
response, and no intermediate subset has been valued.

Largest unknown: the certified regret-versus-width curve and whether a direct
finite-block greedy ordering tracks the exhaustive set-valued teacher.

Cheapest falsifier: source-seal the 2,495-task teacher owner, then retain one
failure-complete invocation and reject the bounded mechanism if no width
through six can satisfy ADR-0323's frozen development conjuncts.

Confidence: high in the qualification and artifact-rebinding result; no
confidence claim is made about the still-unopened width curve or transfer.

## Claims boundary

This result establishes only a candidate-blind h4 fold/call opportunity panel
for later bounded research. It does not select an action width, establish a
best subset, implement or price a finite-block greedy mechanism, model
responder raises or multiway response closure, construct transfer, measure
preparation or a complete 15-second action, integrate a policy or betting
action, cover h32/full ranges or earlier streets, train a blueprint, or
establish NashConv, AIVAT, league, coalition, or poker strength. No revoked
experiment, external publication, or thesis change is authorized.
