## Decision

Accept the first and only ADR-0327 invocation. It completed all 2,495 ordered
certified-v2 tasks across ADR-0326's exact 16-context development panel with 2,495
accounted public HiGHS-DS calls, no consumer or numerical rejection, and no
staging witness left behind. Commit the exact 4,975,258-byte canonical artifact
and the additive solver-free `pontius.fresh_action_width_teacher_result`
rebinder.

The rebinder reconstructs every task from the sealed schedule and rederives
each request, public-state, legal-raise-set, and compiled-LP identity. It checks
all 2,495 canonical endpoint/gap records, independently recomputes every signed
and nonnegative regret interval, payoff-span normalization, interval-max
teacher envelope, strict-dominance survivor set, reporting-only equivalence
set, unique-survivor field, and observation/width/context/campaign digest. Its
tests patch both public consumer and HiGHS solver entry points to fail if
reached. Rebinding therefore opens no new value and makes no solver call.

The exhaustive curve answers only the width-matched teacher question. Width
three is the first teacher width whose full-regret maximum and mean upper
endpoints pass ADR-0323's `0.005` and `0.001` conjuncts. It is not the selected
development width: the direct closed finite-block greedy recovery and
width-matched teacher-excess conjuncts remain unopened and retain full veto
power. No post-outcome threshold, shortcut price, transfer construction, or
runtime election is authorized.

The inherited authority remains explicit. ADR-0318 binds HiGHS 1.12.0;
ADR-0319 requires one public HiGHS-DS call per canonical task; and All 177
ordered observations pass under ADR-0320, leaving the separate consumer
eligible. ADR-0321 preserves the caller-owned legal fallback, while ADR-0322
returns research evidence or rejection with no action. ADR-0324's unopened
transfer remainder stays value-unopened; ADR-0325 authorized its qualifier
exactly once; ADR-0326 retained the exact panel; and ADR-0327's exhaustive
bounded development-teacher is the only value owner invoked here. None of
those controls is weakened.

## Consequences and next boundary

The exhaustive teacher phase is complete. Its result strongly supports a
small-menu hypothesis on the declared reduced game and preserves a large
runtime-choice plateau at wider widths. It does not establish that width three
is produced by the preregistered scalable mechanism, survives fresh transfer,
fits a live action ledger, or handles opponent raises and six-player play.

The active boundary returns to source-only work. Implement and source-seal the
direct closed finite-block greedy development owner before its first price or
candidate-value invocation. Each proposed own raise must carry exact incumbent
and augmented request identities plus complete fold/call response-row closure;
the smallest-raise tie break and width-matched teacher-excess calculation must
be frozen before values. Transfer remains forbidden until the complete greedy
mechanism and its commit-derived seed are sealed.

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** exact artifact bytes, canonical payload, terminal completion,
  schedule/source/panel provenance, 2,495 public-call count, and every rebuilt
  nested identity and interval reduction.
- **Reproduced without solving:** all request, public-state, legal-set, LP,
  task, regret, normalization, envelope, survivor, and result digests.
- **Observed on the qualified h4 panel:** the full-regret curve, survivor
  cardinality distributions, no empty actual equivalence set, and the first
  full-regret-only passing width of three.
- **Diagnostic only:** 21.480207-second whole-command wall time.
- **Unopened:** every closed finite-block price, greedy choice, greedy recovery
  and teacher excess, selected width, transfer context/value, capacity ledger,
  and production integration.
- **Rejected:** treating the first full-regret pass as a selected mechanism,
  ranking plateau members, allowing milliseconds to edit the teacher set, or
  treating campaign wall time as live decision latency.

Supporting acceptance: the first terminal artifact is complete, canonical,
and independently rebinds under a solver tripwire; every frozen teacher
arithmetic and provenance invariant survives.

Opposing evidence: this remains a two-live-seat h4 river fold/call game. The
curve contains no opponent raise response, multiway interaction, full range,
earlier street, runtime capacity, or poker-strength evidence.

Largest unknown: whether the direct closed finite-block greedy order reproduces
the exhaustive width-three quality without excess regret and with at least
90% conservative aggregate recovery.

Cheapest falsifier: source-seal the exact greedy owner, then run its first
development campaign once; any response-row closure defect, typed rejection,
or failure of a frozen conjunct rejects the bounded greedy mechanism without a
threshold relaxation.

Confidence: high in artifact identity, result rebinding, and the reduced-game
teacher curve; moderate that width three is a useful mechanism target; low
that this result alone predicts production action width or poker strength.
