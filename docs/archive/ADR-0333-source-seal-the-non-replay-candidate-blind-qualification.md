## Decision

Accept the source-only candidate-blind qualification boundary for the sealed
ADR-0332 non-replay population. The exact 192-task schedule processes each of
the 96 contexts in order, complete kernel integer universe first and anchored
raise width two second. It inherits ADR-0323's conservative certified regret
interval, payoff-span normalization, separately typed nested-value reversal
allowance, normalized opportunity floor, and ambiguity guard. It stops at the
first 16 qualifying contexts or at the first pool-exhaustion, ambiguity,
nested-value reversal, typed consumer rejection, or unexpected-exception
condition.

No replacement sizing value was opened. The prospective artifact does not
exist at this boundary. The next successor may make only the one retained
qualification invocation through the public no-clobber wrapper after this
source and its exact identities are committed.

The evidence owner makes the ADR-0322 consumer call in exactly one source
location. Accepted evidence binds the exact scheduled request, public state,
kernel legal set, raise-to-to-increment mapping, compact LP, exact opening
policy, responder fold/call actions, certified endpoints, one public-call
count, consumer source, and protocol. It additionally retains the raw
inequality-multiplier hint. The solver-free rebinder independently evaluates
the exact behavioral policy for the lower endpoint and reruns the
outward-rounded bounded-variable certificate for the upper endpoint. Generic
hash validity therefore cannot manufacture a value endpoint without a valid
policy and dual witness.

The inherited authority remains explicit. ADR-0318 binds HiGHS 1.12.0;
ADR-0319 requires one public HiGHS-DS call per canonical task; All 177 ordered
observations pass under ADR-0320, leaving the separately bounded consumer
eligible. ADR-0321 retains caller-owned legal fallback, and ADR-0322 returns
research evidence or rejection with no action. ADR-0323's interval arithmetic
and payoff-span semantics remain unchanged; ADR-0324's prior development pool
remains value-unopened and ADR-0325's invocation ownership remains exactly
once. ADR-0326/ADR-0327 preserve the exhaustive bounded development-teacher
chain, and ADR-0328 only opens the later direct closed finite-block greedy
question. ADR-0330 permanently closes the failed greedy campaign. ADR-0331's
append-and-fsync contract and ADR-0332's exclusive `xb` open exclusively
govern this replacement's population and durability.

## Exact candidate-blind schedule

The schedule digest
`132513efa7cdf32298e5d19cb60afa69fda2580a8e98c9b3e4724781d07a50b2`
binds 192 immutable tasks. For context `i`, task `2*i` is the complete
kernel-derived integer raise-to universe and task `2*i+1` is exactly its
minimum/maximum-anchored width-two subset. The schedule owns no teacher subset,
greedy transition, transfer context, preparation artifact, action constructor,
or production resolver.

For accepted full interval `[L_f,U_f]` and accepted width-two interval
`[L_s,U_s]`, the only regret direction is
`[L_f-U_s,U_f-L_s]`, followed by endpoint-wise clipping at zero for the
nonnegative interval. A context qualifies only when its conservative lower
endpoint is strictly above
`1e-4 * (pot + 2 * effective_stack) + 1e-8 chips`. It is nonqualifying only
when the conservative upper endpoint is strictly below the threshold minus the
separate ambiguity guard. Equality or overlap is ambiguous. Stack alone never
substitutes for the payoff span.

A materially negative nested upper difference has its own
`nested_value_reversal` terminal. It is not relabeled ambiguity, consumer
rejection, or unexpected exception. The schedule, classification, stop, and
execution-phase enumerations are part of the protocol digest and are checked
against their semantic enums before launch.

## Write-ahead invocation ownership

The public wrapper verifies the ADR-0333 source closure, ADR-0332 population,
and exact schedule before exclusive artifact creation. Existing output bytes,
a missing parent, source drift, or schedule drift cause a typed launch
rejection and zero consumer calls. After exclusive creation, the header is
written, flushed, and `fsync`ed. An arm may begin only when the preceding
header or observation receipt has the exact expected record kind and sequence.

Each returned consumer outcome is converted and semantically validated inside
the arm boundary. A nonsemantic return, wrong-task evidence, conversion error,
or owner exception becomes a durable `unexpected_exception` observation with
incomplete invocation accounting; it cannot escape before evidence append.
Every accepted or typed-rejected observation is canonicalized, appended,
flushed, and `fsync`ed before the next arm is authorized. A typed rejection
must reproduce the consumer's exact stage/reason, bound identities, source
availability, and zero-or-one call contract.

An append, semantic-reduction, terminal, close, or final-rebind failure after
exclusive creation is a `QualificationExecutionFailed`, never a launch
rejection. It retains the exact raw journal bytes when readable, generic
verified prefix and untouched suffix, exact last-receipted semantic evidence,
failure phase and task, known durable call count, an explicit unknown flag for
any unreceipted arm invocation, and complete exception evidence. A valid line
that lacked a returned durability receipt does not authorize another call and
is excluded from the durable semantic count. No failure path retries.

The completed terminal is derived only from journal observations. It binds all
evidence digests, derived context outcomes, qualifier identities, stop reason,
known calls, accounting completeness, final observation-line digest, schedule,
pool, source, campaign, exact record count, and synthetic provenance. The
solver-free reader independently reconstructs these fields. It returns a
typed prefix for a nonterminal or torn journal and a result only for one exact
terminal with no trailing byte.

## Verification and defects found before seal

The focused qualification file passes 11 controls; the journal, non-replay,
inherited qualification, and new qualification group passes 39 controls. The
full repository regression passes 1,374 tests in 410.477 seconds with two
expected skips. Ruff is unavailable in the pinned environment (`No module
named ruff`); no substitute linter is claimed.

Adversarial review found and repaired four pre-seal defects:

1. post-launch journal failures were caught by the public wrapper and mislabeled
   as preflight/launch rejection;
2. evidence validation errors could escape after an arm invocation but before
   its observation was journaled;
3. accepted endpoint payloads retained interval values but not the dual hint
   required to independently reconstruct the upper certificate; and
4. nested action-set reversal would have escaped as a generic execution error
   instead of retaining its own semantic terminal category.

The structural repair is one phase-typed execution boundary plus one
witness-rebinding evidence schema, not four message-level exceptions.

## Gates and kill criteria

Kill the source checkpoint on any consumer or solver call over the new pool;
an existing or unsealed output path; a changed task order, target, threshold,
payoff span, or endpoint direction; one semantic control reused as another;
an accepted value without exact policy and dual reconstruction; a rejection
whose stage, reason, identity, or call count is merely plausible rather than
exact; authorization without the preceding post-`fsync` receipt; an
unreceipted arm counted as durable; a post-launch failure labeled preflight;
continuation after any stop; retry; silent suffix repair; generic hashes used
as campaign authority; synthetic values reported as research; or any teacher,
greedy, transfer, preparation, action, h32, earlier-street, latency, or strength
claim.

For the next checkpoint, require this exact source seal in a clean committed
worktree and an absent prospective artifact. Invoke only the public retained
wrapper once. Whatever terminal or infrastructure state occurs becomes the
sole ADR-0333 qualification evidence. Do not delete, overwrite, resume, or
retry it. A target result may authorize only a separate source-sealed panel
artifact/rebinder boundary; it does not authorize teacher values in the same
commit.

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** the source closure, population, 192-task order, classifier,
  receipt rule, evidence schema, failure types, output path, and synthetic
  control identities are exact and value-free.
- **Reproduced:** all inherited arithmetic directions and stop distinctions;
  every target fixture prefix; representative torn suffixes; generic-hash
  semantic corruption rejection; independent policy/dual endpoint rejection;
  and observation/terminal failure reduction.
- **Observed:** no real ADR-0331 qualification arm, qualifier, value, panel, or
  output byte exists at this boundary.
- **Hypothesis:** the new disjoint population contains at least 16 contexts
  whose certified complete-universe regret over anchored width two clears the
  unchanged material-opportunity gate.
- **Rejected:** trusting interval text without its witness, calling all runner
  failures preflight, conflating nested reversal with ambiguity, or treating a
  generic valid hash chain as semantic campaign authority.

Supporting the next invocation: its population, task graph, consumer,
certificate, write-ahead survival, and semantic rebinder are now independently
sealed before values.

Opposing evidence: the result remains an h4, heads-up, river, one-bet,
fold/call-only development qualification. Even a clean target stop says
nothing direct about multiway responder raises, full ranges, earlier streets,
the 15-second action wall, or poker strength.

Largest unknown: whether the replacement pool reaches 16 qualifiers before a
consumer, numerical, ambiguity, reversal, or infrastructure stop.

Cheapest falsifier: the first real complete-universe/width-two pair. Any typed
stop is retained negative evidence and closes the campaign without retry.

Confidence: high in source identity, scheduling, witness rebinding, and
durability semantics; no confidence claim is made about unopened values.
