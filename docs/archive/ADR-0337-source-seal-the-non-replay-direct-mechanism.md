## Certified selection, prices, and gates

For incumbent interval `[L_i,U_i]` and response-closed augmented interval
`[L_a,U_a]`, a candidate's certified finite-block price is only

```text
[L_a - U_i, U_a - L_i]
```

The selected candidate is the greatest feasible behavioral lower endpoint.
An exact endpoint tie selects the smaller nominal raise-to total. Certificate
upper endpoints, price endpoints, reporting tolerances, elapsed time, and
teacher values cannot break that tie.

After widths three through six complete, the owner recomputes the five
inherited development conjuncts from retained semantic evidence:

1. maximum normalized full-regret upper at most `0.005`;
2. mean normalized full-regret upper at most `0.001`;
3. conservative aggregate recovery lower at least `0.90`;
4. maximum normalized width-matched teacher-excess upper at most `0.001`; and
5. mean normalized width-matched teacher-excess upper at most `0.0002`.

Full regret and teacher excess use conservative interval directions. Each chip
quantity is normalized only by `pot + 2 * effective_stack`. Aggregate recovery
sums chip endpoints across all 16 contexts before division; it never averages
per-context ratios. The selected development width, if any, is the smallest
width three through six passing all five gates. Stored booleans never replace
the arithmetic.

- **Unopened:** all 376 replacement direct-mechanism values and prices, the

- **Absence claim:** no counterpart research artifact exists at the prospective
  direct-mechanism path as checked at this source boundary.
- **Rejected:** reviving ADR-0329, treating synthetic width three as evidence,
  selecting from upper endpoints or milliseconds, averaging recovery ratios,
  calling graph edges invocations, retrying after a stop, or reporting command
  wall as live latency.

Supporting acceptance: every possible semantic branch is frozen before value,
the realized adaptive branch is journal-authorized one call at a time, and the
complete success serializer has already survived independently.

Opposing evidence: this remains an h4, two-live-seat river-opening game with a
fold/call-only responder on a qualification-selected target panel. It is not a
representative six-max population and includes no responder raise.

Largest unknown: whether the real response-closed greedy path reaches the
width-three teacher gate with at least 90% conservative recovery and within
both teacher-excess limits.

Cheapest falsifier: make the sole retained 376-call invocation. Any source
drift, exclusive-create collision, typed stop, failed witness reconstruction,
or failed frozen conjunct rejects the bounded mechanism without relaxation or
retry.

Confidence: high in the sealed graph, dynamic call authority, durability, and
interval/gate reductions; moderate that width three will pass the direct
mechanism; low that this reduced result alone predicts production action width
or poker strength.
