## Decision

Accept the first and only ADR-0340 public transfer-qualification invocation.
From clean commit `e1c6c14630e3e8cf72be0a40db80b85eed549b58`, with the
frozen prospective path absent, it reached the preregistered target after 47
complete contexts and 94 accepted one-call arms. Retain the exact append-and-
`fsync` JSONL journal and seal a solver-free semantic result owner plus the
exact 16-context target-only panel.

The journal contains 96 canonical records: one header, 94 observations, and
one terminal. Its terminal reports 16 qualifying and 31 nonqualifying contexts,
zero ambiguity, no nested reversal, no typed rejection, no unexpected owner
failure, complete invocation accounting, and `target_reached`. Contexts 0
through 46 were opened in the sealed complete-universe-then-width-two order.
Contexts 47 through 95 remain unopened. There was no retry, resume, overwrite,
alternate backend, post-stop arm, or second public invocation.

This is a transfer qualification result, not the width-three transfer result.
It establishes only that the untouched ADR-0339 population supplied 16
contexts with a certified material complete-universe advantage over anchored
width two. It neither evaluates nor confirms the frozen development width
three. It cannot select another width, soften a conjunct, create an abstention
policy, or establish a production action lattice.

The partial-result interpretation remains unchanged after value. A later
width-three confirmation passes unrestricted transfer only if every frozen
development conjunct passes on this exact panel. Mean-only, 14-of-16, or any
other partial passage is diagnostic and rejects unrestricted transfer. Any
abstention mechanism remains a new hypothesis requiring a new preregistration
and untouched evidence.

## Consequences and next boundary

The transfer qualification phase is complete. It makes only a source-sealed
transfer-confirmation owner eligible. That successor must take this exact panel
and apply the already frozen ADR-0338 width-three context-local mechanism
without selecting width again. It must freeze its complete-universe comparator,
exact request schedule and reuse rules, conservative envelope directions, all
unchanged development conjuncts, durable evidence schema, typed stops, and
no-retry rule before its first value.

The successor confirms unrestricted width-three transfer only if every frozen
conjunct passes. It may reject width three, but it may not elect width four,
reinterpret a mean-only or 14-of-16 result as success, or derive an abstention
policy from this evidence. No confirmation value is eligible in this checkpoint.

In parallel, value-free responder-raise semantics, a label-free full-width
capacity preflight, blueprint-kernel and abstraction engineering, and the v0a
legal/interface chassis remain eligible under ADR-0340's boundaries. Blueprint
training still requires the five named seals, including confirmed action-lattice
identity and an operational slice-certification audit pipeline. Repeated-actor
multiway closure and cross-street belief/certificate handoff remain research
risks with no existence proof in the current stack.

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** exact journal bytes, invocation source commit, task prefix,
  campaign and terminal identities, public-call accounting, qualifier
  membership, panel identity, and unopened suffix.
- **Reproduced without solving:** accepted policy lower bounds, outward upper
  certificates, regret directions, classifications, stop state, terminal,
  target-panel membership, panel digest, and numerical diagnostics.
- **Observed on the untouched transfer population:** 16 qualifiers in 47
  complete contexts, 31 nonqualifiers, zero ambiguity, and no typed or
  infrastructure failure.
- **Unopened:** contexts 47 through 95; every width-three transfer-confirmation
  value and conjunct; capacity, action, integration, and strength evaluation.
- **Rejected:** rerunning the closed qualifier; treating qualification yield as
  width-three confirmation; selecting a new width on transfer; treating 94 arm
  calls as a complete decision or latency result; or turning systems behavior
  into a decision-quality prior.

Supporting acceptance: the exact preregistered first-16 target was reached
under the durable one-call-per-receipt contract, and every retained endpoint,
terminal, and panel identity independently rebinds with all value-producing
paths disabled.

Opposing evidence: this remains a two-live-seat h4 river opening whose responder
has fold/call only. Opportunity-conditioned panel yield can differ from
population performance. It contains no responder raises, repeated-actor
multiway closure, full range, earlier street, complete 15-second decision, or
poker-strength result.

Largest unknown: whether the frozen ADR-0338 width-three context-local mechanism
passes every unchanged development conjunct on this untouched panel.

Cheapest falsifier: source-seal that exact transfer-confirmation owner, then
make its sole retained invocation. Any source drift, typed rejection,
certificate ambiguity, durability failure, or failed frozen conjunct rejects
unrestricted transfer without relaxing this result.

Confidence: high in exact journal retention, semantic rebinding, invocation
accounting, and target-only panel identity; moderate that this panel can test
the frozen mechanism; low that this reduced-game result predicts production
action width or poker strength.
