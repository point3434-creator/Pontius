## Question

Can the exact directional derivatives of the complete best-response face be
computed without enumerating its Cartesian product, while retaining both
total-function and reachable-support cardinalities and still detecting a row
that is inactive at the source but crosses later on the ray?

This checkpoint asks only whether the mechanism and its label-free controls
are source-sealed. It does not ask what the active face looks like on the legal
h4 target, whether that target completes, whether the mechanism fits the
15-second action clock, or whether it improves a poker decision.

## Decision

Add `exact_directional_face_oracle`. At one exact rational scale on a frozen
one-seat sequence-realization segment, it represents the active response face
as the product of each response information set's exact locally maximizing
actions. It never forms the Cartesian product of response tapes.

Run two independent lexicographic backward passes over the explicit finite
tree. Both passes first maximize exact current response value. One then
minimizes the endpoint-minus-source response slope and the other maximizes it.
The passes own separate selections and caches. They may choose different
actions upstream because those choices change which downstream response
information sets are reached; one shared selection cannot certify both
extrema.

Keep the point and ray instruments distinct:

- the new face oracle reports the exact value and slope interval of the active
  face at one scale; and
- the existing exact sequence-form normal-fan mapper partitions the complete
  ray into cells and facets, considering rows that are strictly suboptimal at
  the source but can cross later.

`compose_exact_directional_face_fan_section` joins them. It rechecks the fan's
maximum affine gain against the face value at every boundary and open-cell
witness, requires every open segment to retain a covering cell row, and
requires every selector-changing boundary to expose a non-singleton face with
strictly separated minimum and maximum gain slopes. The face oracle is not
permitted to certify a ray by itself.

No legal h4 runner, configuration, or result path is added here. The
ADR-0352 public runner remains permanently closed and its 961-byte rejection

## Why the two passes are exact

Only the declared acting player's realization plan changes along the segment.
Against any fixed pure response tape, exact terminal expectation is therefore
affine in the segment scale. At a response information set, ordinary backward
induction maximizes the current affine value. Restricting the secondary choice
to exactly value-maximizing actions and then minimizing or maximizing slope is
lexicographic backward induction over the same perfect-recall dependency
order. Thus the two root slopes are the one-sided directional extrema over
the complete active face without materializing its pure-tape members.

The implementation independently compares every factor's exact maximizer set
and the root value with `exact_best_response_trace`. It also requires the
current profile utility to equal the exact source/endpoint affine line before
subtracting its slope from the response-slope interval. Response and gain
slopes remain separate quantities.

This proof is scoped to the explicit finite perfect-recall game and a
one-player sequence-realization segment. It is not a generic degenerate-LP
sensitivity theorem, a multi-seat perturbation result, or a scalability
claim.

## Two cardinality columns

The total-function cardinality is the arbitrary-precision product of every
information set's exact maximizing-action count. It remains the conservative
semantic count even for entries on dead branches.

The reachable-support cardinality is a separate exact quotient. A sequence-
forest dynamic program sums the allowed upstream choices and multiplies the
child counts reached by each choice; information sets with zero positive
chance/opponent counterfactual support contribute one. It therefore removes
phantom downstream completions without replacing total-function identity or
changing the active face.

Both integers are reported with their bit lengths. Neither saturates, elects
a representative, or gates the other. The repeated-actor control obtains four
total-function tapes but three reachable-support tapes and matches exhaustive
enumeration. A separate zero-support chance control obtains four and two.

## Measurable no-materialization contract

The work ledger records tree nodes and edges, response-action edges, sequence
variables, information sets, each pass's continuation evaluations, action
score terms and action-choice inspections, cardinality additions and
multiplications, exact-integer bit lengths, and materialized response tapes.

Each pass must perform exactly one continuation evaluation per explicit tree
node, one score term per response-action edge, and one action inspection per
sequence variable. Cardinality arithmetic must stay within the frozen linear
logical-operation ceiling. `materialized_response_tapes` must be exactly zero.
The 30-level binary control exposes `1,073,741,824` total-function tapes and
31 reachable-support tapes over 61 explicit tree nodes without constructing
one response-tape product. Increasing the face by a factor of 64 in a paired
scaling control increases reported logical work by less than threefold.

“Linear” here means logical operations in the explicit tree representation.
Exact big-integer operation cost is not constant; the ledger exposes both
cardinality bit lengths so a later capacity diagnostic cannot hide that cost.
The default 100,000-node bound is an explicit-state safety guard, not a face-
cardinality bound. The existing 256 bound in the composed call is a normal-fan
piece bound, not the rejected ADR-0352 tape-product bound.

## Seam and conformance controls

The engineered future-crossing game freezes a row that is suboptimal at the
source by a known exact margin and whose closing slope crosses at scale `1/2`.
The composed system, not just either component, must find that facet, expose
two active total and reachable responses there, report gain-slope extrema
`-1/2` and `1/2`, and cover both neighboring open cells. This control prevents
the point oracle from erasing a future row or the fan/face seam from dropping
the crossing.

The shared `tie_semantics_conformance` registry makes controls mechanical for
every named consumer of the tie contract. Its current closed set is:

- selector-window v2;
- the exact selector fan;
- the old bounded exact tie-aware envelope as a synthetic reference only;
- the tie-aware affine adapter;
- the exact directional-face oracle; and
- the composed fan/face section.

The registry assigns parallel and separating source ties, positive-measure
ties, the future-crossing seam, repeated-actor total/reachable identity, and
high-cardinality no-materialization as applicable. A missing consumer,
missing required control, or unexpected consumer rejects. Invoking the old
bounded envelope on tiny synthetic controls does not reopen its legal h4
runner or revive its rejected integration authority.

Additional exhaustive controls compare values, active cardinalities,
reachable quotients and both slope extrema against every pure response tape
at five rational scales. A positive-measure exact tie remains unresolved even
when its minimum and maximum slopes coincide. Explicit tree and fan-piece
bounds fail closed.

## Float64 qualification

ADR-0351 proved that its Float64 selector calls did not detect source ties. It
did not prove structural selector uniqueness. `Fraction.from_float` preserves
the exact binary value already held by a double; it does not recover an
intended rational value. Float64 can split a structural equality through
rounding noise or collapse distinct rationals to one representable value.
Consequently Float64 tie counts are uninformative in either direction about
structural uniqueness.

A future exact-anchor replay of historical selectors may be preregistered as
low-priority historical curiosity. It is not required for this source seal,
does not reopen an earlier result, and is not evidence against the validity of
the retained Float64-as-computed artifacts.

## Claims boundary

This is mechanism evidence from synthetic exact games. It establishes that
the committed implementation passes its bounded conformance controls. It does
not certify the implementation for arbitrary games, and it records no legal
h4 active-face count, row, value, time, or selector conclusion. It provides no
full-width capacity, action-clock, multiway response closure, strategy quality,
or poker-strength evidence.

The source seal binds the parent decision, both implementation files, both
control files, and the protocol. It includes no h4 fixture value or result
artifact. Any edit to those bound sources requires a new decision rather than
quietly inheriting ADR-0354's authority.
