## Retained target

The target is controlled seat 3 holding `Ks Td` on river board
`2c 7d 9h Js Qc`. The exact one-seat belief carries five complete opponent
axes with widths 1,225 preflop, 1,081 flop, 1,035 turn, and 990 river. At the
river it represents 950,990,049,900,000 Cartesian assignments and
361,767,238,232,400 compatible assignments in compact 79,208-byte unary form.
Compact belief size is not topology size.

For the current semantic 3/3 split, controlled seat 3 is the singleton in the
right half. The left half therefore stores every labeled compatible assignment
of three opponent pairs from 45 cards:

```text
C(45,2) * C(43,2) * C(41,2) = 733,055,400 records
```

The right half stores the singleton plus two opponent axes and therefore
893,970 compatible records. The exact persistent numeric-array lower bounds
are:

| Necessary object | Bytes | Decimal GB |
|---|---:|---:|
| base topology | 249,485,611,328 | 249.486 |
| reverse/open addition | 187,948,252,800 | 187.948 |
| bidirectional topology | 437,433,864,128 | 437.434 |
| optimistic scalar ordering | 202,627,279,808 | 202.627 |
| resident belief cache | 14,678,987,408 | 14.679 |

These are lower bounds. They exclude Python containers, construction
temporaries, incidence key sets, unknown workspaces, sparse CPU/GPU operators,
showdown automata, tensor trains, contraction results, and scratch.

## Admission result

All five resource admission conjuncts are false:

- the 202.627 GB optimistic scalar topology exceeds the fixed 48 GB host
  persistent-numeric cap;
- the 437.434 GB bidirectional topology exceeds that cap;
- the bidirectional topology plus the 8 GB reserve exceeds the observed
  40,974,659,584 available host bytes;
- the 14.679 GB resident belief exceeds the fixed 12 GB device numeric cap;
  and
- the resident belief plus the 2 GB reserve exceeds the observed
  15,641,608,192 free device bytes.

The rejection is not sensitive to one marginal live-memory sample: the
optimistic scalar lower bound alone is more than four times the fixed host cap,
and the bidirectional lower bound is more than nine times it. Changing the
hero side cannot rescue this representation.

The artifact records zero target topology builds, scalar contractions,
priming contractions, and warm contractions, with a null target workload. The
pre-allocation target path took 0.045583 seconds and the complete campaign took
1.064294 seconds. Those walls describe a guarded rejection and are not solver

## Successor direction

The binding defect is representation, not arithmetic or GPU kernel throughput.
The next gate should eliminate card-conflict factors without materializing the
Cartesian product of per-infoset or per-half assignments. Candidate exact
mechanisms include card-factor variable elimination, subset/complement dynamic
programs, or another factor graph whose persistent state scales with hand/card
features rather than `733,055,400 * 64` half tables.

Before any new full-width target, the successor must preregister:

1. exact reduced-case agreement with dense enumeration and current FactorTT;
2. forward and reverse/open-mode semantics needed by the resolver;
3. pre-allocation work and memory formulas with excluded classes explicit;
4. adversarial card-conflict and multi-component controls;
5. CPU/GPU placement and warm-cache ownership;
6. a literal full-width capacity terminal under the same workstation and
   15-second action contract; and
7. no strategy-quality or truncation conclusion from systems passage alone.

Certified support truncation remains a separate mechanism hypothesis. It may
be tested later only after defining an exact discarded-mass-to-chip-value
certificate. This result supplies no such certificate and cannot choose its
threshold or support policy.
