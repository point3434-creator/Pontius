Thirteen new tests pass. They cover full-width arithmetic without allocation,
reject-before-CuPy and live-memory traps, exhaustive bounded colex ranks, an
independent exact recurrence proof, complete/read-only fixture geometry,
static no-atomic/no-incidence source, independent tolerance fields, exact

All five engineered mutations are detected: wrong recurrence divisor,
reversed inclusion-exclusion sign, skipped sixth query label in the adjoint,
dropped source-seat factor, and duplicated sunk payoff. Maximum-card-conflict,
zero-weight, tie-strength, source-permutation, and query/source refresh cases
are present in the complete natural or frozen control lanes.

During implementation, the first current-stack diagnostic compared raw
unnormalized numerator and partition fields and rejected. The existing
`FactorizedCardBelief` rescales unary factors and preserves value through a
common normalization, so those raw scales are not invariant. Their normalized
chip values agreed. Before this seal, the diagnostic was corrected to compare
the invariant numerator/partition ratio and require positive partition. No
tolerance was changed. This is a semantic-unit correction, not a favorable
gate relaxation; the invalid raw comparison and corrected value are recorded
here to prevent recurrence.

The load-bearing 41-test quotient, automaton, FactorTT, and capacity group
passes on the final source. A whole-repository discovery immediately before

## Claims boundary

ADR-0372 establishes one complete ten-card CUDA mechanism and reduced phase
measurements on the named workstation. It spot-checks NVRTC/CUDA behavior on
this platform rather than certifying all devices or future driver versions.
It does not establish a staged scaling curve, 45-card live admission, literal
full-width throughput, 14-second compute passage, one resolver iteration,
solve, complete action, decision quality, support truncation, or poker
strength. A clean bounded result is not a systems prior for the target.
