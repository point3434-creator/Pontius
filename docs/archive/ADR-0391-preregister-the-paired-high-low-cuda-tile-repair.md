## Logical and physical tile contract

The logical feature space is unchanged: global states 0 through 174 and reach
at global feature 175. Only the physical representation changes:

```text
tile 0    logical [  0,  64)    physical [0,128)
tile 1    logical [ 64, 128)    physical [0,128)
tile 2    logical [128, 176)    physical [0, 96)
```

For local logical feature `j`, physical column `2*j` is its high component and
`2*j+1` its low component. Tile 2 owns reach at physical columns 94 and 95;
physical columns 96 through 127 are inactive and poisoned in controls. Global
feature start, logical width, physical active width, and physical stride are
different semantic types. In particular, 352 high/low component visits are
176 features, not a wider game.

The two full 64-feature tiles and final 48-feature tile reuse the one existing
128-column recurrence, compatible, query-covector, and unique-adjoint
workspace in sequence. No second recurrence table or full matrix may appear.
The sole predicted device-peak change is replacing two scalar-result slots
with eight fixed per-tile high/low and reach slots: 9,910,940,332 becomes
9,910,940,380 named bytes. This arithmetic is prospective and must be
independently rederived from source before any CUDA import or allocation.

## Pair arithmetic contract

A pair denotes the exact binary-rational value
`Fraction.from_float(high) + Fraction.from_float(low)`. It remains unevaluated
through source products, zeta recurrences, signed target extraction, fold,
query covectors, adjoint recurrences, fused source contraction, and global
reduction. Collapsing `high + low` is reporting-only after every gate has been
computed from the pair value.

The CUDA source must implement and separately test `two_sum`, explicit-FMA
`two_product`, pair renormalization, add, subtract, pair-times-Float64, and
pair-times-pair. All high-high, high-low, low-high, and low-low products and
their explicit error terms are retained before the final two-component
renormalization. Ordinary contraction is disabled; explicit `fma` is the only
two-product authority. Fast math, flush-to-zero, Float32, TF32, tensor cores,
and approximate intrinsics are forbidden. Source-seal evidence must report
the observed exponent envelope and reject unexpected nonzero subnormal,
nonfinite, or overflow intermediates.

The primitive suite precedes the operator campaign. Its independent
`Fraction` reference covers signed zero, adjacent ULPs, large-plus-small,
near-total cancellation, mixed signs, observed extreme exponents, three-factor
source products, nonzero low-low products, and the engineered ADR-0390
one-ULP residual. TwoSum and TwoProduct must be exact under their frozen finite
preconditions. High-only, dropped-low, swapped-pair, omitted-low-low,
fast-math, and single-pass mutations must reject. Every logical tile and every
operator stage must exhibit detecting nonzero low-lane mass.

## Fold, adjoint, and reduction ownership

ADR-0389's global offsets, complete six-label groups, reach ownership,
normalize-once fold, 90 source pairings, 57 reverse terms, and forward death
before adjoint birth remain unchanged. The repair cannot buy precision by
changing the operator.

No new record-sized scalar arrays are allowed. After one compatible row has
been completely consumed, the existing numerator and reach chunks hold the
numerator high and low; physical columns 0 and 1 of that consumed compatible
row hold reach high and low. After a unique-adjoint row has been completely
consumed, its physical columns 0 and 1 hold the source-contribution high and
low. Direct samples must be captured before either overwrite.

Contributions enter one global online binary tree in increasing global record
or source-rank order. Chunk cuts may neither reset nor reassociate it. Pair
carries use only already-owned dead recurrence rows or consumed complete
covector rows. Tile partials are stored by logical tile ordinal and combined
in canonical order 0, 1, 2 regardless of execution order. Normal and reversed
tile executions must therefore produce byte-identical partial and final pair
results; default and alternate chunks and repeated snapshot/restore executions
must also be byte-identical. Atomics, library reductions, racy in-place trees,
and per-chunk final rounding remain forbidden.

## Exact scalar authority

The bounded controls capture every forward and transpose contribution pair
before reduction, in global order. A host reference that does not import the
device helpers sums each component with `Fraction.from_float`. That gives the
exact binary-rational sum of the represented contribution stream. The device
pair reducer must match its corresponding exact stream within the original
absolute ceiling, and the exact reconstructed forward and transpose streams
must satisfy both original absolute and relative conjuncts. The same two
conjuncts independently apply to the final device forward and transpose pair
values.

This exact reconstruction diagnoses the represented arithmetic and reducer;
it is not exact ground truth for the unrounded linear operator. Complete
ten-card `Fraction` rows and selected independent 25-card direct source,
forward, fold, and adjoint rows remain the operator authority. `math.fsum`,
Decimal, Float128, or one collapsed Float64 may be reported but cannot decide
the gate.

## Bounded campaign and kill criteria

The complete ten-card population retains 210 source occupancies, 210 query
occupancies, 1,260 labeled records, small nonzero-offset chunks, both tile
orders, and every logical and physical boundary. The complete 25-card
population retains 177,100 source occupancies, 12,650 query occupancies, and
75,900 labeled records under the original default and alternate chunk
families. Its device/host numeric caps remain 1/2 GB and its population wall
remains 180,000 ms on the exact frozen RTX 5080/CUDA 13.2/CuPy 14.2 runtime.
The entire laboratory source seal remains bounded by 600,000 ms.

Reject on any original semantic or numerical failure, any byte difference
across chunks, repeats, or tile orders, any failed error-free primitive,
missing low-lane detecting mass, hidden full workspace or spill-backed reserve
failure, changed compile option, tile width, chunk, or tolerance after an
outcome, or any need to inspect a 45-card value to choose or repair the
mechanism. A failure is retained; it is not retried with a more favorable
arithmetic contract.
