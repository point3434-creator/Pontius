## Same compiler and exact executed object

CUDA 13.3 NVCC is the sole compiler path. The complete argv grammar, ordered
options, temporary-source and output positions, `sm_120` target, and exact
UTF-8 CUDA source hash are frozen. Environment-supplied options, fast math,
NVRTC fallback, and post-compile suffix repair are forbidden. One compile
contains all arms, so no arm receives a friendlier compiler or container.

Compiler stdout and stderr, return code, and the direct ELF cubin are bounded
raw evidence. The cubin is appended and fsynced, and its complete evidence is
ACKed, before any external inspector or module load. `cuobjdump`, `nvdisasm`,
and the CUDA module loader must receive the same exact bytes. This prevents a
repaired inspection object from standing in for a different executed object,
the defect that made the 0403--0415 chain expensive.

The literal device identity is not guessed from this machine today. It is the
already retained ADR-0416 row: RTX 5080, compute capability 120, driver 13030,
runtime at least 13020, 17,094,475,776 total bytes, 84 multiprocessors, and
1,536 maximum threads per multiprocessor. Device geometry is exact; driver
13030 and runtime 13020 are compatible minima, so a later compatible driver is
not mislabeled hardware drift. The future child queries identity once and
fails typed before scientific launch on an exact-geometry or minimum-version
mismatch. This preregistration performed no such query.

Sixteen `extern "C" __global__` entry names are frozen: eight positional and
eight RRNS encode, recurrence, selective, streamed-global, and contraction
entries. No additional global entry may hide an ungated resource bill. Every
name must resolve and carry all four resource instruments; device helpers are
inlined into those rows. Resident and batched RRNS share the same entries and
differ only in their frozen channel ranges and schedule.

## Resource quantities stay typed

The ptxas verbose log must name, per kernel, registers, stack-frame bytes,
spill-store bytes, and spill-load bytes. Both spill-byte ceilings are zero.
`cuobjdump --dump-resource-usage` separately supplies `REG`, `STACK`, `LOCAL`,
and `SHARED`; driver functions separately supply registers, local and shared
bytes, and maximum block width. Effective registers and backing use the frozen
maxima across instruments, with ceilings 255 registers and 4,096 stack-plus-
local bytes per thread.

`nvdisasm` reports static local-memory instruction sites as a diagnostic.
Static sites are not dynamic traffic; ptxas spill bytes are not stack/local
backing; stack/local backing is not an exact spill count. Missing, malformed,
or duplicated fields reject rather than becoming zero or borrowing another
instrument. This directly retires the semantic-quantity substitution exposed
by ADR-0395.

## Exact memory liveness, not a byte pile

Literal 45 remains geometry only. The preflight reuses ADR-0438's exact byte
rows at feature tile 64 and chunks 4,096, then assigns every device buffer a
stable identity, size, birth, and death boundary. A sweep over those events
derives peak liveness independently. Forward and adjoint workspaces do not
coexist. The batched second pass may overwrite the drained first-pass table
workspace and reread the same captured input, but may not call that reread a
second allocation or omit its traffic. Aliasing requires identical storage and
nonoverlapping live intervals.

The frozen implementation choice is resident rank-to-cards, with no resident
rank-to-mask or materialized adjacency and no dense output. An arm passes the
symbolic memory conjunct only when exact peak live bytes plus the 2,000,000,000-
byte reserve are at or below the retained device total. Equality passes; one
byte over fails. This is a symbolic memory admission, not an actual-45
allocation, execution, or latency result.

## Reduced exactness and fair walls

Only ADR-0438's already-open complete-10 and signed-12 populations run. A
common host authority constructs each fixture once. Each arm receives one
warmup and three timed repetitions under both ascending and descending colex
orders, with the arm order rotated across repetitions. Warmup, allocation,
transfer, every launch and replay, reconstruction, exact verification, and
cleanup remain charged. Compile and inspection are charged to the laboratory
but excluded from arm-to-arm execution comparison.

Every candidate-population observation is the exact partition of twelve named
phases by consecutive `perf_counter_ns` boundaries. Device work synchronizes
before its closing host boundary. CUDA-event times are reporting only. The sum
of the twelve intervals must equal the observation wall exactly. Nine global
phases plus every interleaved candidate row similarly sum exactly to the
laboratory wall; public elapsed minus laboratory elapsed is a separately
charged launch/transport envelope. Gaps and overlap are therefore arithmetic
failures, not reviewer judgments.

The frozen walls are 30 seconds for all timed repetitions of one population-
arm pair, 60 seconds for compile plus resource work, 240 seconds for the
laboratory, 30 seconds outside the laboratory, and 270 seconds for the public
process. All comparisons are exact integer `<=`: one nanosecond below and
exactly at a ceiling pass; one over fails. These are validation-invocation
walls. They are not per iteration, per solve, actual-45, street, or action
latency.

Exact verification begins at family admission, pair encoding, and labeled-
query aggregation, not after hidden host preprocessing. It covers aggregates,
both recurrences, selective and streamed-global coordinates, numerator,
reach, divisibility, terminal rounding, and binary64 bits on both orders and
all repetitions. RRNS uses working-CRT range plus redundant-channel base
extension; every one-changed-channel mutation must be detected without retry
or correction. A consistent all-channel wrong codeword must pass RRNS and fail
the unbounded differential, preserving the correlated-fault boundary.
Device entries emit fixed-width limbs or residues only. Host big integers are
permitted solely after transfer in the named, charged reconstruction and
independent-verification phases; they may verify an arm but may not masquerade
as work performed by its device operator.
