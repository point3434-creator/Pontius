## Deterministic reduced fixtures

The calibration axes are the complete Cartesian product of five card domains,
four topology arms, two runtime modes, three base types, two refresh states,
and two arithmetic schedules: 480 cells. One complete warmup pass and five
complete measured passes make 2,880 scientific calls under one public owner.
Every measured value survives; the median is the third sorted integer and the
maximum is retained. Each pass orders its cell keys by a prospectively frozen
SHA-256 permutation, so neither arm order nor a result can choose its neighbor.

The raw H fixture has 176 signed integer components for every rank-zero-through-
four subset. Components zero through 174 come from the frozen SHA-256 formula.
The pricing vector is fixed and ends in one; component 175 is chosen so the
exact dot equals the requested `h(T)`. Positive-witness fixtures have `h=0`.
Prove-none fixtures have `h=-1` at rank one and zero elsewhere. The latter is
the ADR-0456 switch stressor: structural or opaque base is `-721`, and the
mixed base adds `-1` only on the exact sparse master support. Every price is
therefore `-1` or `-2`. Source seal must independently prove the early switch
for every frozen domain/base pair before timing exists.

Positive fixtures use base `+1`; the mixed representation uses structural
`+2` and sparse `-1`, leaving every price positive. A positive-witness return
claims one exact positive coordinate only. Prove-none requires complete exact
or conservatively bounded coverage. Exact argmax and full ties remain
validation outputs, not timed runtime modes.

The lattice constant uses its own rank-zero, scale-720 derivation and exact
reconciliation with the H circuit's scale 24. Mixed support is source ranks
zero, `N-1`, and every rank divisible by 257, with duplicates removed. These
fixtures exercise all three provenance types; none supplies production
identity, exponent admission, or arithmetic width.

## Compiled arithmetic and the RRNS order seam

All four arms and both schedules live in one literal CUDA translation unit and
one compiled, inspected, then loaded cubin under the ADR-0447/ADR-0448
toolchain and runtime identity. Python may own compilation, launches, phase
boundaries, transport, and the durable journal. It may not perform scientific
arithmetic, maintain the priority frontier, compare a bound, test a sign, or
decide the hybrid switch inside a timed call. Prefix and hybrid frontiers are
device-resident.

Positional values use independently derived signed two's-complement limb
widths plus one sign-extension guard. RRNS preserves the eligible five-then-
four replay: four working channels plus redundancy, drain/reuse, then the
remaining four working channels. Every pass is charged.

RRNS residues have no signed order. Before any bound enters a heap, any price
is compared with zero, any maximum or tie is decided, or any switch predicate
is tested, the device must reconstruct the symmetric signed working-range
integer into a separately width-derived positional decision key. Redundant
base extension and the semantic range check precede use. Reconstruction and
key storage are charged to the RRNS wall and memory ledger. A host bigint
comparator is forbidden. This closes the otherwise hidden asymmetry between a
residue evaluator and an ordered branch-and-bound algorithm.

All workspaces are allocated before warmup. Raw H is campaign-resident. Each
call rebinds the pricing-vector epoch and charges the H contraction. A cold
base call transfers and rebuilds the typed base artifact and bounds. An exact
hit validates every producer, payload, structural, correction, support, and
epoch digest before reuse. Cold and hit remain separate distributions.

## Frozen fit and materiality rule

For each phase/arm/mode/base/refresh/schedule cell, use the maximum of the five
measured walls at each of the five domains. Fit

`wall = a + b * phase_work`

with exact rational arithmetic, `a >= 0`, and `b >= 0`. Candidate solutions
are the feasible unconstrained least-squares solution, the `b=0` boundary, the
`a=0` boundary, and zero. Minimum exact SSE wins; ties choose the larger target
projection, then larger intercept, then slope. A constant work coordinate uses
zero slope and the largest observed response. Add the maximum positive residual
and multiply the target prediction by `5/4`, then ceil once per phase. The
primitive upper is the sum of those nineteen integer ceilings. The independent
reader reimplements this rule rather than importing the projector.

Target work is symbolic combinatorics only. Direct and zeta use exact geometry;
positive all-price witnesses use algebraically fixed work where available;
prove-none prefix uses the complete prefix-tree worst case; and hybrid uses
prefix work through the hard checkpoint plus complete zeta. No 45-card H row,
base, price, allocation, or kernel is formed to discover a friendlier pruning
fraction. The resulting symbolic-45 number is a primitive capacity diagnostic,
not measured target latency.

The preregistered speed question is narrow. In prove-none mode, zeta's
projected primitive upper must be at most half direct's under the same synthetic
base, refresh state, and arithmetic schedule, conjunctively over all twelve
such cells. Domain medians, maxima, and ratios are all reported but cannot
replace that test. Failure rejects the material-zeta speed claim, not exact
algebra. Success still cannot select a production topology while the base and
consumer frequencies are absent.
