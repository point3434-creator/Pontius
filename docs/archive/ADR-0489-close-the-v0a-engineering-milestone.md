and an authoritative two-hand run before useful poker development can continue?

## Decision

The accepted library and driver source checks establish its engineering acceptance;
the completed rehearsals retain their non-evidentiary standing. This is not an
operational result, a trained policy, full v0a research completion or poker strength.

closure and one-shot authorization. Do not implement the proposed operational
launcher or versioned host/trace slice merely to repeat the fixed controls under
another label. Open only the portable-blueprint artifact brief and design below.
All other parked lanes stay parked. This disposition takes effect at its separately
authorized decision commit; the working draft or generated STATUS cannot adopt it.

## Prospective execution and dependency clarification

ADR-0485 already separates contract/source work from operational opening, and
ends the bootstrap at source seal plus completed rehearsal report. Apply that
distinction explicitly to future v0a task briefs:

1. Ordinary correctness work has declared expected behavior and scoped test
   authority. It may iterate under the existing isolated-snapshot policy without
   inventing a one-shot experimental owner or an authoritative result. A source
   task still needs its brief, applicable review, tests and specific commit approval.
2. Rehearsal remains a separately authorized, non-evidentiary lifecycle/cost check.
   It is not a fit, ranking, selection, strength claim or correctness acceptance.
3. Operational or experimental work still requires every applicable preregistered
   prerequisite, required measured limit, source seal and one-invocation authority.
   Purpose and actual data use determine the category, not a filename or mode flag.
   Calling a campaign a test cannot bypass these requirements or revive an owner.
4. A blocked prerequisite names its consumer and protected property. Missing
   operational budgets block operation, not an independent source-only task.
   A required safety limit is never waived; its necessity is established for the
   actual operation before a missing measurement is treated as its admission blocker.
5. Parked work is not automatically unparked. Each new task names the precise
   capability opened and its stop rule. No additional work is implied by this closure.

This clarifies the existing bootstrap and dependency rules; it does not amend
research evidence standards, accepted trace modes, source immutability, Python
support, snapshot testing, failure retention, or the historical owner closures.
Separating future authorization records from semantic traces remains an option
for a later named consumer, not an adopted interface or implementation permission.
