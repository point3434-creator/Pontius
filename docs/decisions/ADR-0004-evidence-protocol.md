# ADR-0004: Evidence and disagreement protocol

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-08-18.

## Decision

Consequential recommendations must state confidence, opposing evidence, a
falsifying experiment, and a kill criterion. Negative results remain recorded.
Technical assessments do not change to accommodate either collaborator's
preference, though they must change when evidence changes.

## Reason

The project combines high uncertainty, long feedback loops, and attractive
complex ideas. Agreement bias and forgotten reasoning are material risks.
