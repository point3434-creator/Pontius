# ADR-0023: Preregister the transparent post-probe screen

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-19

## Decision

Freeze `experiments/rules/river-post-probe-scheduler-screen-v1.json` before
measuring the production shadow feature. The screen has six rank-aggregation
scores, five discrete macro-allocation options, and fixed checkpoint-four DCFR
as an explicit fallback. It fits no continuous weights, tree, neural model, or
threshold.

Every adaptive candidate first pays checkpoint two. It begins from fixed
checkpoint four, promotes high-scored contexts to checkpoint six or eight, and
funds them by demoting low-scored nonrecipients to checkpoint two. The allocator
may use less work but cannot exceed fixed checkpoint four in either aggregate
iterations or deterministic alternating state visits.

Select a candidate separately on four development folds and evaluate it once on
the fifth. Board group is the fold unit, so related range families never split.
The selection objective is aggregate raw exploitability improvement over fixed
checkpoint four. Payoff-normalized quantities remain score inputs and
diagnostics, but raw chips are the allocation objective because state pot sizes
are part of computation value.

Charge a score that uses the shadow statistic for its measured update overhead,
one-pass feature summary, and allocation decision. A score that omits the shadow
pays only its active summary and decision cost. Negative paired timing deltas
are floored at zero per context. The deterministic state-work comparison is
primary for reproducibility; aggregate measured reduction per millisecond must
also beat fixed DCFR.

## Why this family

On the already revealed development trace, raw DCFR positive regret is a much
better scalar for raw future improvement per state visit than its normalized
form (`0.618` versus `0.227` Spearman). Conversely, normalized efficiency is
associated with smaller raise-to-pot geometry. The frozen family therefore
contains raw active, raw shadow, fused raw, and three normalized-plus-geometry
scores. Rank aggregation prevents utility scale from silently becoming a
tunable coefficient.

Policy movement is omitted. It adds policy construction and comparison work,
and its raw target correlation is only `0.194`. The candidate family is already
large enough at 30 adaptive rules. Adding learned weights after observing the
shadow feature would turn a falsifiable screen into a development sweep.

The CFR+ shadow is not a standalone CFR+ solve. It applies CFR+ numerical updates
to regret deltas generated by the active DCFR trajectory. A unit falsification
shows equality after the first iteration but divergence during the second
alternating iteration. Its only valid interpretation is a cheap statistic of
the paid DCFR path.

## Gates

The cross-validated selection procedure passes only if all of the following
hold:

1. Its chosen candidate strictly improves raw final exploitability over fixed
   checkpoint four in every held-out development fold.
2. Aggregate uplift captures at least 25% of the exact post-probe perfect-
   information uplift.
3. Aggregate iterations and deterministic state visits do not exceed fixed
   checkpoint four.
4. Aggregate raw reduction per charged measured millisecond strictly exceeds
   fixed checkpoint four.

Failure retains fixed checkpoint-four DCFR and prohibits reserved-board
construction. Passing authorizes only freezing the all-development winner in a
new commit; it does not authorize looking at validation or test before that
freeze.

## Dissent protocol

**Confidence:** high that the rule is causal and auditable; moderate that
five-fold selection can distinguish a small uplift; low that any river-only
gain transfers to six-player earlier streets.

**Opposing evidence:** the perfect post-probe ceiling is only 5.44% for DCFR,
the new shadow signal is unmeasured at freeze time, and source wall-clock fields
exclude existing feature extraction. Conservative explicit charging can erase
an otherwise real strategy improvement.

**Largest unknown:** whether the shadow accumulator contains incremental
ranking information after conditioning on active regret and bet geometry.

**Cheapest falsification:** run the exact 1,024-context development shadow probe
and the frozen group cross-validation once. Do not amend this family from its
result.
