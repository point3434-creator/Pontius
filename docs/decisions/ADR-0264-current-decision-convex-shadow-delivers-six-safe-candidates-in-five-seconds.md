## Decision

Accept the prospective decision-aligned shadow result. The convex spine now
has exact-safe, value-positive, within-ledger evidence at the player actually
facing the decision, not only at a future wide-axis responder.

Authorize only a separate label-blind post-fold posterior preregistration. Use
the same six sources and positional schedule, replace the one structurally
frozen observed call with a fold, and require fresh identities and the same
current-player/downstream topology before any strategy label. Do not tune the
factor, interior floor, materiality threshold, cut budget, or schedule from
this result.

## Claims boundary

This is a fixed six-context prospective shadow result on retained boards and
blueprints, all after one observed call. It is not an IID success rate. No
fresh fallback is measured, no candidate is emitted, and no deployment,
fallback-dominance, global-optimality, composition, cross-street,
multiplayer-global-safety, exploitation, or broad poker-strength claim is made.
