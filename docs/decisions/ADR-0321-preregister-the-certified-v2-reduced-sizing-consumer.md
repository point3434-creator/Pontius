- Front-Door-Process: ADR-0321

- Front-Door-Revoked: ADR-0281
- Front-Door-Active-Next: Implement and canonical-LF source-seal only the additive ADR-0321 certified-v2 reduced-sizing research consumer and adjacent seal; bind the exact public state, fold/call-only response model, exact joint probabilities and showdown signs, a caller-supplied ordered kernel-legal raise-to subset with mandatory minimum and fully contestable maximum anchors, and the distinct raise-to-to-increment conversion; verify ADR-0318 source/runtime before one counted public HiGHS-DS proposal; return certified endpoints/policy/responses or a failure-complete typed rejection requiring the caller-owned legal fallback; run only unsealed analytic/differential/corruption controls and edit no v1 consumer, v1-v4 owner, retained artifact, adapter, allowance, or live action spine
- Front-Door-Blockers: the ADR-0321 consumer source and seal do not exist; no exact legal-action binding, typed consumer attempt record, or caller-owned fallback integration exists; the fold/call-only reduced game is not a production six-max decision model and cannot emit a betting action; no fresh action-width value, exact regret curve, own-column/opponent-row closure, h32/full-range result, complete 15-second decision ledger, or strength result is authorized

## Question

What is the narrowest consumer contract that can use ADR-0318's now-validated
certified solver without reviving a rejected action candidate, confusing legal
raise-to totals with reduced-game bet increments, or presenting a heads-up
fold/call model as a six-player action policy?

## Decision

Implement one new additive research consumer after this preregistration is
committed. It may evaluate a caller-supplied subset of exact legal integer
raises in the canonical one-bet river game. It may not choose, emit, or apply a
`BettingAction`, enter the Legal Decision Spine, import the historical native
consumer, or name any v1-v4 candidate owner.

ADR-0320 remains the eligibility authority: All 177 ordered observations pass
with one public HiGHS-DS call each under the frozen HiGHS 1.12.0 runtime. This
keeps the separately preregistered consumer eligible. This ADR consumes that
bounded eligibility without broadening its finite evidence.

The consumer is an untrusted-proposer boundary. Before its first backend call
it must verify the committed ADR-0318 adapter/dependency seal, the pinned
runtime identity, and its own separately committed source manifest. It then
permits exactly one counted call to SciPy's public HiGHS dual-simplex interface.
There is no retry, alternate backend, native fallback, relaxed allowance, or
partial solution path.

## Exact supported game shell

The request owns an immutable `NoLimitBettingState`, its exact current legal
decision, exact positive rational joint private-state probabilities, exact
showdown signs in `{-1, 0, 1}`, the fixed response-model identity, and an
ordered tuple of nominal raise-to totals. Its semantic digest excludes any
human label and includes every game field above.

The consumer accepts only a deliberately narrow shell:

- river, exactly two live seats, and the current actor faces no wager;
- check and at least one raise are legal, while fold and call are not;
- the exact maximum legal raise-to equals the separately retained maximum
  contestable raise-to, so every modeled wager can be called in full;
- the public pot is positive and even, as required by the existing integer
  half-pot reduced payoff convention; and
- every supplied raise-to is a strictly increasing member of the kernel's
  inclusive legal interval, with the exact minimum and maximum retained.

The request must state whether it supplies the complete legal integer universe
or a strict restricted subset. The consumer independently checks that claim.
This scope field prevents a bounded ladder from being reported as the oracle.
Maximum contestable and own all-in remain separate semantic fields even though
this narrow shell requires them to coincide numerically.

## Nominal amount conversion

Define the kernel call-base total as

`base_raise_to = street_contribution + call_amount`.

For each nominal legal raise-to total `r`, derive a distinct nominal reduced
bet increment `b = r - base_raise_to`. Derive `minimum_bet` and effective
`stack` only from the corresponding minimum and maximum increments. The
consumer must never pass a raise-to total directly as an LP bet size, use the
remaining stack merely because it matches an increment in a fixture, or
collapse the maximum-contestable and own-all-in meanings. The accepted result
retains both ordered tuples and their one-to-one mapping.

## Result and rejection contract

On success return one immutable accepted record containing:

- the semantic request, public-state, legal-set, consumer-source, and compiled
  LP identities;
- exactly one public-backend invocation;
- the ordered raise-to totals and distinct reduced bet increments;
- the exact normalized opener policy aligned as check followed by those
  raises, and the responder's fold/call best actions;
- the feasible behavioral lower bound, outward certified upper bound, signed
  and nonnegative certificate gaps; and
- the complete ADR-0318 certified solution as diagnostic evidence.

On unsupported state, stale action set, source/runtime drift, backend
exception, malformed return, certificate rejection, or post-solve binding
failure, return a typed immutable rejection. It records the stage, stable
reason, exception module/type/message/cause chain, whether the public backend
was invoked, and the available request identities. It contains no candidate
action and explicitly requires the caller to use its own already-legal
fallback. `KeyboardInterrupt`, `SystemExit`, and other non-`Exception`
control-flow signals are not converted into strategy evidence.

## Frozen controls before any fresh value

Only unsealed analytic, differential, and corruption tests may exercise the
implementation before its source seal is committed:

- exact raise-to membership, ordering, anchors, full-versus-restricted scope,
  maximum-contestable separation, stale-state, multiway, facing-bet, odd-pot,
  and non-river rejections occur before a backend call;
- nonzero street contribution proves raise-to totals and bet increments are
  not interchangeable;
- accepted toy calls match direct ADR-0318 output field for field and make one
  public call;
- backend exception and malformed primal, multiplier, status, objective,
  nonfinite, or certificate evidence return typed rejection after one call;
- source or runtime drift returns typed rejection before any call;
- static imports exclude native simplex, the legacy reduced-sizing consumer,
  every v1-v4 source/owner, action emission, and result artifacts; and
- the historical adapter, runner, retained artifacts, and parked candidates
  remain byte-identical.

The consumer and seal source hashes, import closure, protocol digest, and
focused-test hash must be recorded in a successor source-only ADR. No retained
campaign or candidate comparison is part of this checkpoint.

## Kill criterion

Do not seal the consumer if any unsupported semantic shell reaches HiGHS; any
raise-to passes as an increment without explicit conversion; a restricted set
can claim complete scope; any supplied amount is not kernel legal; an accepted
record differs from the certified adapter; a corruption is accepted; a
rejection retries, invokes native code, or owns a betting fallback; source or
runtime drift reaches the backend; or the module can emit/apply an action.

## Consequences

This boundary deliberately dissolves one misleading production question. A
certified solution of the one-bet fold/call game can support exact reduced-game
action-width diagnostics, but it cannot by itself determine a live poker
action because an actual responder may raise and a six-player node may have
more than one opponent. Production action selection therefore remains blocked
on a game model that includes those response rows and closes them against
own-action columns.

After a passing source-only successor, the next eligible work is an
artifact-free integration control proving exact context/action binding and
caller-owned fallback behavior. Only then may a wholly fresh action-width
preregistration define reduced-game regret, alternating column/row closure, and
population transfer. None of those values is opened here.

## Claims boundary

This ADR freezes a prospective software and evidence contract only. It does
not implement a consumer, validate a source hash, invoke HiGHS, evaluate a
candidate set, choose an action width, establish exact ladder regret, price a
column, close an opponent response, integrate a resolver, emit a legal action,
fit a 15-second complete decision, cover h32/full ranges or earlier streets,
train a blueprint, or establish AIVAT, NashConv, league, coalition, or poker
strength. No revoked experiment, external publication, or thesis change is
authorized.
