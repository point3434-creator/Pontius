## Question

Can Pontius answer the own-action-width question without reviving a fifth fixed
ladder, treating a reduced cost under stale opponent rows as finite action
value, or using a two-player fold/call toy as a production six-player policy?

## Decision

Open one new additive research line: certified finite-block action-width
research. Its local authority is the ADR-0322 certified-v2 consumer applied to
an exact kernel-derived complete integer raise-to universe and to anchored
subsets of that same universe. It may not import or rename any v1-v4 action
owner, tune a pot fraction, emit a betting action, enter the Legal Decision
Spine, or make a capacity or poker-strength claim.

This is not action-abstraction v5. It asks two separate questions:

1. how much certified reduced-game chip value is lost at each bounded number
   of raise actions; and
2. whether a context-local finite-block selection procedure can find nearly
   the best subset without enumerating every subset in a later, wider owner.

The exhaustive teacher answers the first question. A direct certified greedy
procedure is the first untrusted proposer for the second. Reduced-cost or
specialized pricing remains downstream until it reproduces direct finite-block
evidence and carries the exact opponent-row identity under which it was priced.

The inherited trust chain remains explicit. ADR-0318 binds HiGHS 1.12.0, and
ADR-0319 requires one public HiGHS-DS call per canonical task. All 177 ordered
observations pass under ADR-0320 and keep the separate consumer eligible.
ADR-0321 leaves every legal fallback with the caller-owned legal fallback
boundary, while ADR-0322 returns research evidence or rejection with no
action. This protocol narrows none of those controls.

## Exact reduced-game scope and semantic types

Every retained context is an ADR-0322-supported state: river, two live seats,
unopened check-or-raise decision, equal effective stacks, positive even pot,
minimum full raise of two chips, and an own maximum equal to the maximum
contestable total. The complete legal raise-to universe is rederived from
`NoLimitBettingState.legal_decision()` as every integer in the inclusive kernel
interval. No pot-fraction source, historical lattice, or caller-supplied legal
surrogate may enter.

Keep these meanings nominally distinct even where fixtures coincide:

- `PrivateRangeWidth` is four private types per seat;
- `RaiseActionWidth` counts raise-to actions only and excludes check;
- `KernelRaiseToTotal` is a total street contribution;
- `ReducedBetIncrement` is the ADR-0322 converted wager;
- `CertifiedChipRegretInterval` is chip-valued research evidence;
- `NormalizedOpportunityFloor` is dimensionless;
- `NestedValueReversalAllowance`, `TeacherEquivalenceAllowance`, and
  `QualificationAmbiguityGuard` are separate chip-valued controls; and
- `OpponentResponseRowSetIdentity` names semantic response rows, not an LP row
  number, constraint count, dual vector, or action-set digest by coincidence.

The fixed reduced response model remains heads-up fold/call only. For each
admitted raise and responder private type, the semantic response-row set
contains exactly one fold and one call row. This is complete only for this
declared reduced model. Responder raises, later actions, multiway coupling, card
removal across six ranges, and earlier streets remain absent.

## Prospective value-free structures

After this ADR is committed, implement one standalone value-free module. It
must use a new local SHA-256 counter stream, core card evaluation, the exact
betting kernel, and ADR-0322 nominal request types only. Static imports must
exclude historical action abstractions, prior action-width pools and panels,
legacy/native sizing oracles, HiGHS adapters, result modules, replay, resolver,
blueprint, preparation, clock, and action construction/application.

Freeze this development source contract:

- baseline commit `a8f2ce3c0af9282625f13e6f0d8e93ebd57c0fa6`;
- development seed text
  `pontius|adr-0323|certified-finite-block-width|development|baseline=a8f2ce3c0af9282625f13e6f0d8e93ebd57c0fa6`;
- 96 ordered contexts, four private hands per seat, positive exact joint
  support with integer weights from one through nine, pots drawn from
  `(6, 10, 14, 20)`, effective stacks drawn from `(8, 10, 12)`, and a two-chip
  minimum full raise;
- a structural sign filter requiring both `-1` and `1`, at least three distinct
  opener rows, and at least three distinct responder columns; and
- exact legal universes of respectively 7, 9, or 11 raise-to totals, with no
  duplicate semantic context.

The source must expose exact anchored-subset schedules without solving. Width
two is minimum plus maximum. For widths three through six, enumerate every
lexicographically ordered combination of interior legal totals plus those two
anchors. Check is always present in the later LP but is not counted in
`RaiseActionWidth`. The exact per-context schedule sizes are therefore
`C(N-2, w-2)` for universe size `N`; the module must verify that identity and
freeze aggregate work before any value owner exists.

The transfer population is not constructed at this boundary. Its future seed
text is deterministically derived only after the complete greedy mechanism
source and its source-seal ADR are committed:

`pontius|adr-0323|certified-finite-block-width|transfer|mechanism-commit=<40-lowercase-hex>`.

The transfer generator must later reuse the frozen chip ranges, width, and
structural filter but must have zero semantic counterpart in the development
pool and the maintained prior-context inventory. This commit-derived rule is
the reservation; no transfer card, context, or value may be constructed while
the mechanism remains unfrozen.

## Candidate-blind qualification before subset values

After a successor seals the value-free source and development-pool digest,
source-seal a separate owned qualification runner before invoking it. Process
the 96 contexts in order. For each context call only ADR-0322 on:

1. the complete kernel integer universe; and
2. the anchored width-two subset `{minimum, maximum}`.

Every call must return accepted evidence with exactly one public HiGHS-DS
proposal, the expected request/legal/LP/source identities, and a certificate
interval no wider than ADR-0318 permits. Any rejection, drift, missing call, or
changed ordering rejects the run and leaves no qualified panel.

For a full interval `[L_f, U_f]` and subset interval `[L_s, U_s]`, retain the
raw signed difference interval `[L_f - U_s, U_f - L_s]` and the conservative
nonnegative regret interval

```text
[max(0, L_f - U_s), max(0, U_f - L_s)].
```

Reject if nested action sets reverse by more than a separately typed `1e-8`
chip allowance. Qualify only when the regret lower endpoint is strictly above
`1e-4 * (pot + 2 * effective_stack) + 1e-8 chips`. Classify nonqualifying only
when the upper endpoint is strictly below the threshold minus the separate
`1e-8`-chip ambiguity guard. Equality or overlap is ambiguous and rejects the
campaign before later values. Stop immediately after the first 16 qualifiers;
fewer than 16 in the complete pool rejects. Freeze their ordered identities
before implementing the exhaustive teacher or greedy value runner.

The payoff span is derived from the reduced payoff shell. Stack alone may not
stand in for it. The normalized floor and both chip-valued guards remain
different fields even though two guards currently have the same numeral.

## Exhaustive bounded teacher and exact regret curve

After the qualified development panel is committed, source-seal one owned
runner before values. For each retained context, evaluate the complete universe
once and every anchored subset at raise widths two through six. Reuse within
that invocation is allowed only by exact request digest. A rejected consumer
result is a complete observation and rejects the campaign; no native fallback,
retry, changed subset, or skipped record is allowed.

For every subset, report the certified chip-valued full-minus-subset interval
using the endpoint arithmetic above and its normalized endpoints using the
derived payoff span. The word `exact` means the exact legal integer universe,
exact rational game input, exact subset membership, and certified chip interval;
it does not mean that a floating HiGHS proposal is a rational exact optimum.

At each width, exhaustive enumeration defines a set-valued teacher. Its value
interval is

```text
[max(candidate lower endpoints), max(candidate upper endpoints)].
```

A subset is certified dominated only if another subset's lower endpoint exceeds
its upper endpoint. Retain all nondominated subsets and name a unique best
subset only when exactly one survives. Subsets whose possible excess loss is at
most a separately typed `1e-8`-chip teacher-equivalence allowance may be
reported equivalent, but that allowance may not select qualification, excuse a
certificate failure, or become an LP tolerance.

## Closed finite-block pricing and greedy proposer

The first mechanism uses direct certified finite differences, not a solver
reduced cost. Begin with the anchored width-two subset. For each omitted legal
raise, record an own-block proposal bound to the exact incumbent subset and its
`OpponentResponseRowSetIdentity`; then add the complete fold/call response rows
for that raise and solve the augmented subset once. Only after this response
closure may the augmented-minus-incumbent interval be called a closed
finite-block price.

Every price record must retain the incumbent and augmented request digests,
legal-set identities, response-row-set identities, proposed raise-to total,
converted increment, certified endpoints, and the explicit
`own block -> opponent response closure -> certified solve` phase order. The
reduced model has no remaining response violation after both fold and call rows
exist for every responder type; that fact must be checked structurally, not
inferred from a zero dual or reduced cost.

At each next width through six, choose the smallest raise-to total among the
candidates with the greatest certified behavioral lower endpoint and repeat.
Retain its possible excess loss against the exhaustive width-matched teacher.
The direct finite-block runner is authoritative for these bounded games. A
future reduced-cost or specialized proposer is eligible only if it reproduces
every uniquely ordered development price/choice within certified intervals,
reports zero undetected mismatch, and alternates own-block proposals with a
response oracle until both violations close. A price under an incumbent row set
is never the finite value of admitting an unclosed block.

## Development selection and fresh transfer

For each development context and greedy width, report maximum and mean
normalized full regret, and conservative aggregate recovery over anchored
width two. Aggregate interval arithmetic uses sums of chip endpoints before
division; it is not a mean of ratios.

The development-selected width is the smallest of three through six whose
conservative upper normalized full regret has maximum at most `0.005` and mean
at most `0.001`, whose conservative aggregate-recovery lower endpoint is at
least `0.90`, and whose greedy possible excess over the width-matched exhaustive
teacher has maximum at most `0.001` and mean at most `0.0002`. If none passes,
reject the bounded greedy mechanism and retain the complete curve as negative
evidence. Do not widen, relax, or choose a post-outcome exception.

Only after the mechanism source, all constants, exact selection rule, result
schema, and development runner are source-sealed may the transfer seed be
materialized. Build a separate 96-context pool, apply the identical
candidate-blind full-versus-width-two qualification, retain the first 16
qualifiers, and freeze that panel before any greedy or intermediate-width
transfer value. The already selected width must then pass the same four
conjuncts on transfer. Development chooses; transfer only confirms or rejects.

## Capacity and production boundary

No reduced-game quality pass is a 15-second fit. Only after transfer may a
separately preregistered capacity experiment build preparation artifacts. Each
artifact must bind the exact public state, range/payoff input, legal universe,
response-row set, mechanism source, and solver/certificate identities. Under
ADR-0307, report preparation construction, hit validation, misses,
invalidations, aborts, live work, certificate work, synchronization, emission,
and the fixed 1,000 ms reserve. Preparation never enlarges the continuous
15,000 ms action wall.

Production remains blocked until a later owner includes responder raises and
multiway response generation, integrates through the one-seat convex master
and exact Legal Decision Spine, covers h32 and full ranges, and passes a
complete action-clock ledger. This ADR authorizes none of those steps.

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** ADR-0322 provides a source-sealed, one-call, exact-context consumer
  for the declared fold/call-only shell; the kernel provides the legal integer
  interval.
- **Reproduced:** none at this preregistration boundary.
- **Observed:** no fresh structure, value, timing, or action is opened here.
- **Hypothesis:** context-local closed finite-block selection can reach a small
  raise width with little certified reduced-game regret and can transfer to one
  untouched h4 population.
- **Rejected:** fixed-ladder v1-v4 revival, stale-row reduced cost as finite
  block value, and reduced-game systems correctness as production evidence.

Supporting the experiment: complete legal universes are enumerable at the
frozen chip depths; direct subset solves give a stronger local authority than a
heuristic ladder comparison; and exhaustive teachers make a later scalable
pricing proposer falsifiable.

Opposing evidence: the game has only four private types, one bet, one opponent,
and no responder raise. A dynamically excellent size set here may fail once
response rows, future streets, and multiway ranges are admitted.

Largest unknown: whether the width curve and greedy ordering survive the
response-side expansion that the production game requires.

Cheapest falsifier: after temporal source and panel seals, run the 16-context
development teacher and reject if no width through six meets the frozen gates.

Confidence: high in the evidence separation and bounded exact enumeration;
moderate that the greedy proposer will approximate the exhaustive teacher; low
that a fold/call h4 result alone predicts production action width.
