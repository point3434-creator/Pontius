`pontius.fresh_action_width_structures` module, its adjacent immutable seal, and

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** source and dependency bytes, deterministic pool identity, exact
  kernel universes, semantic context uniqueness, and combinatorial work.
- **Reproduced:** the pool digest, 440 attempts, chip distributions, 96 unique
  sign matrices, and 12,556 subset count through focused controls.
- **Observed:** no timing or sizing result.
- **Hypothesis:** the unopened pool contains at least 16 unambiguous material
  full-over-width-two contexts under ADR-0323's frozen interval rule.
- **Rejected:** interpreting structural diversity or work count as evaluation
  power, action-width quality, latency, or poker strength.

Supporting acceptance: all legal amounts come from the maintained six-player
kernel, the module cannot solve or act, and the exact combinatorial bill is
known before qualification.

Opposing evidence: structural sign diversity does not guarantee material
sizing opportunity, and h4 one-bet contexts remain narrow poker models.

Largest unknown: qualification yield under the certified-v2 consumer.

Cheapest falsifier: source-seal the owned qualifier, then process the frozen
pool in order and stop on its first ambiguity/rejection or after 16 qualifiers.

Confidence: high in the structure/provenance result; low that structure alone
predicts later quality.
