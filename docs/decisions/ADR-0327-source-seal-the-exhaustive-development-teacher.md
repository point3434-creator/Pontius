## Decision

Accept and source-seal the additive `pontius.fresh_action_width_teacher` owner
before its first development-panel invocation. It binds ADR-0326's exact
16-context panel to one immutable schedule of 2,495 certified-v2 tasks, owns
all conservative interval reductions and terminal states, and cannot emit or
apply a betting action.

No width-three-through-six value was opened while implementing, testing, or
sealing this owner. The only accepted solver values used by focused controls
are a tiny two-private-type schema toy outside the development panel. Schedule
construction rebinds requests and LP identities but never calls the public
consumer.

The next boundary is the one retained development-teacher invocation. It is
not a greedy price, action-width selection, transfer experiment, capacity
measurement, six-player response model, or production integration.

The inherited authority remains explicit: ADR-0318 binds HiGHS 1.12.0;
ADR-0319 requires one public HiGHS-DS call per canonical task; and All 177
ordered observations pass under ADR-0320, leaving the separate consumer
eligible. ADR-0321 preserves the caller-owned legal fallback, while ADR-0322
returns research evidence or rejection with no action. ADR-0324's unopened
remainder stays value-unopened, ADR-0326's exact panel remains the only input,
and its exhaustive bounded development-teacher boundary is the source now
sealed here. None of those controls is weakened.

`0998564a13428bfea300086011a9c5bce649ff3ec1bd00d20a632992dcd43f45`.

typed numerical rejection. Unexpected exceptions preserve the exact completed
context prefix, current task, known public-call count, and the fact that the
count is incomplete.

## Certified teacher semantics

For full interval `[L_f, U_f]` and subset interval `[L_s, U_s]`, each subset
retains signed regret `[L_f-U_s, U_f-L_s]`, its nonnegative clipping, and both
endpoints divided by the context-derived `pot + 2 * effective_stack` payoff
span. Stack alone never substitutes for payoff span.

At one raise width, candidate intervals `[L_i, U_i]` reduce to teacher interval
`[max_i L_i, max_i U_i]`. Candidate `i` is certified dominated only when some
other candidate has `L_j > U_i`. All survivors remain in the nondominated set;
a unique best subset exists only when exactly one survivor remains. The
nondominated-set cardinality per context and width is therefore a primary
flatness measurement: singleton means decisive survival, while larger sets
measure an unresolved plateau under the certificates.

`TeacherEquivalenceAllowance(1e-8 chips)` is reporting-only. A candidate is
reported equivalent only when its conservative possible loss against the
teacher upper envelope is at most that allowance. Equivalence cannot change
the teacher interval, dominance set, unique survivor, certificate acceptance,
or any gate. The equivalence set may be empty when certificate intervals are
wider than the reporting precision.

That empty case corrected an initial unsealed implementation mistake. The
draft asserted that at least one candidate must be reportably equivalent to
its own interval envelope. A synthetic wide-interval control falsified the
assertion: the mathematical maximum is bounded by an envelope assembled from
candidate endpoints, but no individual candidate need be provably within
`1e-8` chips of its upper endpoint. Removing the assertion prevents false
precision. It changes no opened value because the development campaign remains
uninvoked.

Later value-dominance filtering may preserve runtime freedom, but no secondary
criterion is part of this teacher. A future capacity owner may hypothesize
choosing the cheapest menu only inside retained value freedom; milliseconds
cannot edit the teacher set or reach backward into this artifact.

## Result retention and artifact boundary

The source freezes a complete canonical JSON schema for successful, consumer-
rejected, numerical-rejected, and unexpected-runner outcomes. It retains every
subset's exact task and accepted identities, certified endpoints, normalized
regret, width envelope, survivor/equivalence indices, full-minus-teacher
interval, context identity, terminal evidence, and public-call count.

`run_and_retain_adr0323_exhaustive_development_teacher` is the authorized next
invocation path. Before calling the consumer it requires the final and staging
paths to be absent, opens the staging path exclusively, writes and fsyncs an
in-progress marker, and thereby leaves a durable no-retry witness if the
process fails. After a semantic terminal result it replaces that marker with
canonical single-final-LF bytes, fsyncs, hard-links without clobbering the final
path, rereads exact bytes, and only then removes staging. Existing final or
staging state rejects before any consumer call.

This closes R88's known rendered-terminal failure mode before a much larger
result. It does not make a future artifact authoritative by itself. A successor
must pin exact bytes and implement a solver-free rebinder before consuming the
result.

## Consequences and next boundary

The development teacher is now invocation-eligible but value-unopened. The
only authorized next research action is one clean-worktree call through the
no-clobber retained wrapper. A typed rejection is the terminal result, not
permission to repair and rerun. A completed result will measure the complete
reduced-game regret curve and the distribution of plateau versus singleton
teacher sets; it still will not select a scalable mechanism or production
raise width.

Only after exact result bytes and a solver-free rebinder are committed may a
separate successor implement the already-preregistered direct closed finite-
block greedy proposer. Transfer construction remains forbidden until that
complete mechanism and its commit-derived seed are frozen.

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** exact source/protocol/schedule identities, task counts and order,
  conservative reduction definitions, complete result schema, no-clobber
  retention behavior, and focused source-only controls.
- **Reproduced without development solving:** all 2,495 request, legal-set, and
  LP identities plus exact panel membership and combinatorial counts.
- **Observed on bounded schema toys:** accepted/rejected result plumbing,
  endpoint directions, normalization, overlap, empty equivalence, and artifact
  publication behavior.
- **Unopened:** every width-three-through-six development value, the teacher
  curve, plateau distribution, greedy price/choice, and transfer structure or
  value.
- **Hypothesis:** the reduced games have a mostly flat menu landscape with a
  minority of decisive contexts and a useful knee at a small raise width.
- **Rejected:** forced point ranking under overlapping intervals, mandatory
  equivalence at unjustified precision, an equivalence allowance as a decision
  rule, and runtime cost as a teacher-set editor.

Supporting acceptance: the schedule and arithmetic authority are fully frozen
before values, the result type preserves rather than breaks uncertainty, and
the output path is reserved before a future call can open evidence.

Opposing evidence: the exhaustive workload is still a two-live-seat h4 river
fold/call game. Set-valued honesty cannot make its response model predictive of
six-player raises or earlier streets.

Largest unknown: the actual certified regret curve and whether the direct
finite-block greedy ordering later tracks the exhaustive survivor sets.

Cheapest falsifier: run the one sealed campaign and retain its first terminal
artifact; any rejection is terminal, and a completed curve that fails the
frozen ADR-0323 gates kills the bounded mechanism.

Confidence: high in the source, schedule, interval semantics, and artifact
boundary; no confidence claim is made about values that remain unopened.
