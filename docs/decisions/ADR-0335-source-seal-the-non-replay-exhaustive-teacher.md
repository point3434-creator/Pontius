## Certified teacher semantics

For a full value interval `[L_f,U_f]` and subset interval `[L_s,U_s]`, subset
regret is only

```text
[L_f - U_s, U_f - L_s]
```

with endpoint-wise nonnegative clipping for reported regret. A negative upper
endpoint beyond the separately typed chip reversal allowance is the only
`nested_value_reversal` condition. Structural reduction errors are execution
failures and cannot masquerade as that semantic stop.

For candidate subset intervals `[L_i,U_i]` at one width, the teacher envelope
is `[max_i L_i, max_i U_i]`. Candidate `i` is certified dominated only when
some other lower endpoint is strictly greater than `U_i`. Every remaining
candidate survives; a unique best subset exists only when exactly one survives.
The nominal `1e-8`-chip equivalence allowance is reporting-only: candidate `i`
is reported equivalent only when `max_j U_j - L_i <= 1e-8`. The equivalence set
may therefore be empty and cannot select, break a tie, excuse a reversal, or
change a certificate.

Full-minus-teacher regret uses the same conservative endpoint direction.
Chip regret is normalized only by each context's exact payoff span
`pot + 2 * effective_stack`. Stack, legal width, LP dimension, elapsed time,
and certificate tolerance cannot substitute for payoff span. Nondominated-set
cardinality is retained as the plateau measurement. A later runtime-cost rule
may choose only inside value freedom retained by the teacher and cannot edit
the teacher evidence or survivor set.

## Evidence, durability, and independent rebinding

The public owner has one consumer call site. Every accepted real arm retains
the exact scheduled request, public state, kernel legal set, raise-to totals,
bet increments, compact LP, opening policy, responder fold/call actions,
behavioral lower endpoint, raw inequality-multiplier hint, outward certified
upper endpoint, consumer identities, and one public-call count. The reader
reconstructs the exact behavioral policy value and dual certificate without a
solver before accepting either endpoint.

A typed consumer rejection must match its exact stage, reason, bound/source
identities, fallback disposition, exception chain, and zero-or-one call count.
An owner exception or invalid returned object becomes a durable
`unexpected_exception` observation with incomplete invocation accounting.
Consumer rejection, nested reversal, unexpected owner failure, and completed
schedule are distinct terminals. Header append, call authorization, arm
evidence, observation append, semantic reduction, terminal append, close, and
final rebind are distinct execution phases.

Only the exact preceding header or observation receipt, returned after write,
flush, and `fsync`, authorizes the next arm. A post-launch failure returns the
raw journal bytes, generic verified prefix and untouched suffix when readable,
last durably recorded semantic evidence, exact known calls, whether an
unreceipted invocation may exist, execution phase, and full exception chains.
Exclusive `xb` creation prevents overwrite or silent replay.

The execution reducer advances one arm at a time. An initial synthetic control
exposed that replaying the complete prefix between every arm was quadratic and
still unfinished after two minutes; that implementation was rejected before
the seal. The accepted reducer is incremental, while the final reader still
reconstructs the entire journal independently in one linear pass. A second
control exposed Python tuple versus JSON-list header comparison after all
records were durable; canonical-JSON byte comparison now makes the verifier
container-representation independent. These diagnostics opened no value.

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** exact panel and qualification identities, complete 2,113-task
  order, protocol, source closure, prospective path, reduction directions,
  terminal/failure schemas, and synthetic journal identities.
- **Reproduced without solving:** full synthetic serialization, receipt-gated
  continuation, incremental and independent reductions, all 16 synthetic
  context results, prefix/torn-tail recovery, and failure taxonomy.
- **Unopened:** all 2,113 replacement teacher values; every width-three-through-
  six teacher curve; direct mechanism; transfer; capacity; action; and strength.
- **Absence claim:** no counterpart research artifact exists at the prospective
  teacher path as checked at this source boundary.
- **Rejected:** treating synthetic endpoints or command wall as research
  evidence, reusing ADR-0328 values on this panel, calling a structural error a
  nested reversal, replaying after a stop, or promoting a later teacher knee
  directly into a production width.

Supporting acceptance: the exact future task universe and all evidence-
survival and semantic-reduction machinery are sealed before their first value,
including a complete success-shaped journal rather than a rejection-only
fixture.

Opposing evidence: the prospective experiment is still an h4, two-live-seat,
river-opening game with a fold/call-only responder. Exact enumeration may be
useful as a teacher but remains orthogonal to six-player response closure and a
15-second production decision.

Largest unknown: whether the replacement interval teacher again places the
small-menu knee at width three on this non-replay panel.

Cheapest falsifier: make the sole retained invocation. Any source drift,
exclusive-create collision, typed rejection, true nested reversal, unexpected
owner failure, unreceipted append, infrastructure failure, or final semantic
rebind mismatch rejects the campaign without relaxation or replay.

Confidence: high in the sealed schedule, journal survival, call-accounting
schema, and interval/set reductions; low that unopened reduced-game values
predict a production action width or poker strength.
