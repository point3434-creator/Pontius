  solver-free endpoint and interval reduction.
- **Observed on the qualified target-only panel:** first frozen-gate pass at
  width three, median knee at three, positive-lower tail at positions 9 and 11,
  first all-zero-lower width four, and the exact survivor distributions.
- **Reproduced without solving:** policy/dual endpoint witnesses, regret
  directions, payoff-span normalization, set envelopes, aggregate summaries,
  and result identity.
- **Absence claim:** no direct non-replay mechanism result, selected width,
  transfer result, capacity result, or action result exists in this closure.
- **Rejected:** using median as the preregistered gate, calling width three
  uniformly sufficient, ranking plateau members, replaying ADR-0329, dividing
  campaign wall by calls as latency, or treating this as production strength.

Supporting acceptance: the one-shot journal is complete, independently
rebinds under solver tripwires, and the frozen gate and descriptive diagnostics
are now structurally distinct.

Opposing evidence: the panel is qualification-selected for width-two
opportunity, not a representative six-max population. The game remains h4,
two-live-seat, river opening, and fold/call-only for the responder. No opponent
raise, multiway closure, full range, earlier street, live ledger, or strength
measurement appears here.

Largest unknown: whether ADR-0331's direct response-closed greedy mechanism
reaches the width-three teacher gate with at least 90% conservative aggregate
recovery and within both width-matched teacher-excess limits.

Cheapest falsifier: source-seal the complete non-replay direct owner and its
success-shaped durable journal, then make its one retained 376-call invocation.
Any semantic closure defect, typed stop, or failed frozen conjunct rejects the
bounded mechanism without relaxation or retry.

Confidence: high in retained-byte identity, solver-free reductions, and this
panel's curve; moderate that width three is the right direct-mechanism target;
low that this result alone predicts production action width or poker strength.
