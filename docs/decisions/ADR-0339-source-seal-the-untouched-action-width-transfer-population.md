and no transfer quality has been observed. Development width three is frozen
by ADR-0338 and does not enter this generator or its filters.

The later transfer interpretation is frozen now, before qualification value.
Width three confirms unrestricted transfer only if every unchanged
development conjunct passes on the sealed transfer panel. A mean-only pass,
14-of-16 context success, or any other partial pass is diagnostic and rejects
unrestricted transfer. It does not authorize a width-three-with-abstention
policy. Abstention would be a distinct hypothesis requiring a new
preregistration, frozen rule, and untouched evidence.

Only after that source is committed may its no-clobber wrapper be invoked once.

is eligible during qualification. A later transfer result may confirm or
reject development width three; it may not select width four or relax a gate.

## Evidence classification and dissent

- **Known:** exact pre-value commit and seed derivation, source closure,
  protocol, first-96 ordering, cards, exact ranges, legal universes, semantic
  identities, work ledger, and finite comparison inventories.
- **Reproduced without solving:** 478 attempts, structural distributions, all
  ordered context and pool digests, 13,587 prospective subsets, and zero
  counterparts among the exact 1,244 prior keys.
- **Unopened:** transfer qualification, panel membership, every transfer
  endpoint and regret, width-three transfer confirmation, capacity, action,
  and strength.
- **Absence claim:** no semantic counterpart exists in the four enumerated
  finite inventories under the declared cross-family payload.
- **Rejected:** seeding from ADR-0338 outcomes, collision skipping, using IDs
  as non-overlap evidence, converting raise-to to a bet by coincidence, calling
  work counts invocations, selecting another width on transfer, or treating
  structural diversity as decision-quality evidence.

Supporting acceptance: the transfer generator was outcome-blind, its seed was
reserved before mechanism value, the complete first-96 pool is collision-free,
and all legal actions are rederived from the exact betting kernel.

Opposing evidence: this remains an h4 two-live-seat river-opening population
with a fold/call-only future response shell. Finite non-overlap does not prove
representativeness, and no transfer value has yet been measured.

Largest unknown: whether candidate-blind qualification yields 16 unambiguous
contexts and whether the frozen development width three then passes unchanged
quality gates on that panel.

Cheapest falsifier: source-seal the transfer qualifier and process this pool in
order. Any source drift, early value, ambiguity, consumer rejection, missing
durable receipt, or fewer than 16 qualifiers rejects the next stage without
reseed, retry, or threshold change.

Confidence: high in deterministic structure, legality, and finite non-overlap;
no confidence claim is made about transfer quality, production width, latency,
or poker strength.
