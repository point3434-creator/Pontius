## Exact candidate-blind schedule

The schedule SHA-256
`d54267fc71c165637fbf1ea73c7e29be7a6d0471a01396ca788099625087b680`
binds exactly 192 immutable requests. For transfer context `i`, task `2*i` is
the complete consecutive integer raise-to universe returned by the betting
kernel, and task `2*i+1` is its exact minimum/maximum-anchored width-two subset.
The schedule constructor independently rebuilds the ADR-0339 pool and rejects
same-shaped tasks from either development population.

For accepted intervals `[L_f,U_f]` and `[L_2,U_2]`, the only certified regret
interval is `[L_f-U_2,U_f-L_2]`, clipped endpoint-wise at zero after the signed
nested-value check. A context qualifies only when the conservative lower
endpoint is strictly above
`1e-4 * (pot + 2 * effective_stack) + 1e-8 chips`. It is nonqualifying only
when its upper endpoint lies strictly below the threshold minus the distinct
ambiguity guard. Equality or overlap is ambiguous. Stack never substitutes for
payoff span.

The owner stops after the second arm of the first 16 qualifying contexts. It
also stops on the first ambiguity, materially negative nested upper difference,
typed consumer rejection, unexpected exception, or after all 192 observations
without reaching the target. These are distinct nominal terminal types. No
partial stop is skipped, retried, or repaired into another category.

## Evidence and durability ownership

The ADR-0333 evidence codec binds each scheduled task, context semantic
identity, request, public state, exact legal raise set, raise-to-to-increment
conversion, compact LP, source and protocol, exact opening policy, responder
fold/call actions, certified endpoints, and one public-call count. For a real
accepted endpoint, its solver-free validator independently evaluates the exact
behavioral policy for the lower bound and reruns the outward bounded-variable
dual certificate for the upper bound. Generic hashes cannot manufacture a real
endpoint.

ADR-0340 adds an independent outer authority. Exclusive `xb` creation precedes
the header, and its path already disables newline translation. Every header or
observation is appended, flushed, and `fsync`ed before its exact receipt can
authorize the next arm. Invalid owner output becomes a durable
`unexpected_exception` with incomplete invocation accounting. Observation,
reduction, terminal, close, and final-rebind failures remain phase-typed
execution failures with exact raw bytes, verified prefix, untouched suffix,
last-receipted evidence, known call count, and explicit unreceipted-call status.
No failure path retries.

The completed terminal is derived only from retained observations. It binds
every evidence digest, context outcome, qualifying index and semantic identity,
stop, known calls, accounting completeness, final observation-line digest,
pool, schedule, source, campaign, record count, and synthetic provenance. The
reader rebuilds all those fields without a consumer or solver.

A target-only panel may be constructed only from a terminal whose stop is
exactly `target_reached` and whose 16 qualifying outcomes reproduce the 16
indices. The panel contains only pool, schedule, campaign, journal, terminal,
ordered context identities, indices, and provenance. It contains no endpoint,
regret, policy, value, or later menu. Every non-target terminal and every
prefix rejects.

## Strategic sequencing ratification

The integration milestone is now explicitly split. **v0a** is the exact legal
spine plus immutable blueprint lookup through complete hands, including river;
its strategy may be weak and uncertified, while legality, state transition,
clocking, provenance, and fallback remain certified. Its purpose is early
interface discovery. **v0b** replaces the river placeholder only after a
full-width river strategy bridge earns its own seal. Neither milestone is a
strength claim.

Off-tree opponent-action handling is a three-candidate preregistered bake-off:
pseudo-harmonic translation, an interpolated continuous-size action-likelihood
model, and an exact re-solve trigger. An adversarial bettor chooses legal
off-menu integers to maximize posterior corruption relative to an exact ground
truth. Measure belief total variation, downstream certified chip-value damage,
and charged wall time. No incumbent becomes the default without passing.

Blueprint engineering may proceed at the source/value-free level, but training
requires five prior seals: trainer semantics, checkpoint/resume identity,
abstraction identity, transfer-confirmed action lattice, and an operational
slice-certification audit pipeline that can abort pathological training early.
No rental or long training run is authorized by this decision.

Repeated-actor multiway response closure and cross-street belief/certificate
handoff remain research risks with no existence proof in the current stack.
The project has evidence that its method transfers to new questions, not that
these questions are solved.
