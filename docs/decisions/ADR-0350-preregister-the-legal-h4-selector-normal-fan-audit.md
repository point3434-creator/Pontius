## Question

Along four prospectively frozen, downstream-relevant one-dimensional sections
of ADR-0349's unchanged legal h4 acting-policy space, what is the exact normal-
fan geometry of each player's best response? In particular, where is the
source total response tape fixed, where is a selector tie genuinely
unresolved, where has the tape switched, and how does that certificate view
differ from reachable-support behavioral identity?

This question is broader than the old source selector-window proof but still
finite. A best-response value is the maximum of fixed-response affine rows on
a sequence-form realization segment. Fixed tapes define cells, exact ties
define facets or degenerate regions, and the old margin-over-closing-slope
breakpoint is the distance from the source to the first facet along one such
ray. The audit charts exact one-dimensional sections of that normal fan. It
does not infer a multidimensional volume, a population distribution, or a
production action.

## Total-function and reachable-support identity

Two identities are retained as separate columns:

- **Total-function identity** compares every sorted information-key/action
  entry, including entries downstream of branches that are currently
  unreachable. It is the sole certificate authority because a fixed affine
  row and its future reuse require a complete immutable tape.
- **Reachable-support identity** compares only entries reached under positive
  chance/opponent support and the response tape's own upstream choices at the
  current section point. It is an honest behavioral diagnostic. Support added
  and removed, common-live action changes, and currently unreachable total-
  tape changes are reported separately.

An upstream selector switch can make later same-player information sets dead.
Consequently total identity can report a conservative change that reachable
identity calls behaviorally irrelevant, or reachable supports can differ even
when a simple entry count looks benign. Reachable identity is explicitly
reporting-only and cannot pass, fail, enlarge, or shrink a certificate window.

## Exact fan, legacy differential, and envelope direction

For each direction and target player, every selected fixed tape needed to
cover `[0,1]` is scored at both endpoints. Exact local optimality inequalities
produce its closed rational cell. The union must cover the section; open
segments and all cell boundaries are re-solved exactly and classified.

Because all four frozen directions alter one public history, each overlaps the
old affine breakpoint scope. The source cell's exact upper endpoint must equal
the independently computed minimum margin divided by closing slope, to
`Fraction` identity. The additive Float64 primitive is run twice: with zero
allowance it must reproduce that exact breakpoint within `1e-12`; with the
separate `1e-12` selector-margin allowance it must not extend beyond the exact
total-tape source cell. The allowance is a conservative certificate reserve,
not a tie breaker and not a reporting equivalence threshold.

For every fan tape, exact response value and profile utility define an affine
deviation-gain row. At every scheduled point the active exact tape's row must
equal the exact best-response gain, all retained rows must lie at or below
that gain, and their maximum must equal it. Thus best-response gain is the
maximum upper envelope of fixed-tape affine gain rows. A one-seat master that
minimizes epigraph coordinate `z` therefore uses constraints `z >= row`; any
reversed envelope or inequality direction fails the gate.
