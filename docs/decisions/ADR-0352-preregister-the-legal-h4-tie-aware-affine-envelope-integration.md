## Question

Can the valid ADR-0351 normal-fan evidence be integrated without electing one
response tape inside an exact tie? More specifically, can a bounded exact
teacher retain every locally maximizing total tape and its affine gain row,
can a typed adapter reserve selector-window v2 for exact singleton sources,
and can the resulting maximum envelope reproduce the best-response gain with
the master direction `z >= row` while keeping total-function and reachable-
support identity distinct and independently auditable?

This is a same-fixture integration question, not untouched confirmation. It
does not ask whether ties are common at full width, whether the mechanism is
fast inside the 15-second action clock, or whether it improves poker play.

## Decision

Add `exact_tie_aware_affine_envelope`. At every exact fan boundary and open-
cell witness it takes the Cartesian closure of the Fraction oracle's local
maximizing-action sets. Each resulting complete total tape is re-evaluated as
an immutable fixed response and must remain locally maximal and equal the
exact best-response value. The product is bounded before construction; an
overflow or a cross-product tape that is not self-consistent rejects rather
than truncates.

For every retained tape, score the source and endpoint exactly. Sequence-form
realization interpolation makes each fixed-response value affine along the
section. Subtract the exact affine profile utility to obtain one deviation-
gain row. At every critical sample, all active tapes must attain the exact
gain, no retained row may exceed it, and the maximum retained row must equal
it. Tapes with identical intercept and slope remain separately retained and
are additionally reported as an affine-equivalence class. Equivalence is not
permission to erase tape identity.

Add `tie_aware_affine_adapter` with three explicit modes:

- `v2_single_tape` when the exact source active set is a singleton and the v2
  source-separation certificate has positive scale;
- `fail_closed` when the exact source is a singleton but its numerical margin
  does not clear the separately typed reserve; and
- `tie_aware_maximum_envelope` when the exact source active set has more than
  one total tape.

In the third mode, every v2 tape window must be zero. A later slope may not
turn a tied source into a unique certificate. The complete affine row library,
not any deterministic legal-order selector, supplies the value envelope.
This is the tie-aware legal h4 affine-envelope successor to the rejected
selector-window line; selector-window v2 remains the only single-tape path.

## Active-set scope

“Every active tape” here means every Cartesian completion of the exact
selector oracle's locally maximizing actions under its finite perfect-recall
contract. It is not an enumeration of every strategically irrelevant normal-
form completion at zero-reach histories, nor an absence claim about other
representations. Each enumerated completion is independently checked under
its own fixed continuation tape. This is the smallest exact closure that
preserves the selector semantics consumed by the existing fan and affine
machinery.

The maximums are exact `Fraction` comparisons. No epsilon decides whether a
tape is active or whether two rows are equivalent. The `1e-12` selector margin
allowance remains a conservative v2 reserve only. It cannot add or remove an
active row, break a tie, or choose an integration mode.

## Total and reachable identity

Total-function identity remains the sole certificate authority. For every
scheduled current active tape, compare it against every exact source-active
tape. Each pair serializes, in full:

1. the source total tape;
2. the source tape pruned under the current point's positive support;
3. the current total tape; and
4. the current tape pruned under that same current policy and support.

The artifact then reports total identity, reachable identity, total entry
changes, common-live action changes, support additions/removals, and currently
unreachable changes. Thus a solver-free result owner can later rederive both
identities artifact-only. Reachable identity remains reporting-only and cannot
weaken a total-tape certificate or edit the exact active set.

This closes ADR-0351's serialization qualification: the successor no longer
asks a digest to stand in for a missing source-pruned tape.

## Envelope and epigraph differential

For each of the four inherited policy directions and both target players, the
runner retains all exact affine response/gain coefficients and evaluates the
maximum envelope at the unchanged 17-point dyadic schedule. At every point:

- every exact active tape must be present in the row library;
- every active row must equal the exact best-response gain;
- every row must be no greater than that gain;
- `z = max(row)` must give nonnegative residuals `z - row`, with at least one
  zero residual; and
- lowering `z` by a fixed exact positive witness must violate at least one
  active constraint.

These are independent checks of both mathematical directions. Best-response
gain is a maximum upper envelope. A minimized master epigraph therefore uses
`z >= row`; neither `min(row)` nor `z <= row` is admissible.

## Controls and gates

Synthetic controls, run before any public h4 invocation, establish:

- an exact crossing retains one source row, both rows at the `1/2` tie facet,
  and the correct maximum envelope;
- a positive-measure tie retains both total tapes even when their affine rows
  are equivalent, and dispatches only to the tie-aware envelope;
- a repeated actor with two binary tied information sets yields four total
  tapes but only three reachable-support tapes, so the identity types cannot
  collapse; and
- invalid row/tape bounds, missing active score endpoints, source-reserve
  overlaps, and exclusive-path reuse fail closed.

The public gate requires clean Git provenance, the ADR-0351 retained-map and
rejected-certificate state, exact fixture/direction identities, complete
active closure, honest v2 dispatch, zero v2 windows for tied sources, exact
maximum-envelope and epigraph identities, both pruned tapes, total-function
authority, zero production best-response calls, at most 256 fixed-score calls,
Float64/exact row error at most `1e-12`, a 60-second cumulative subject wall,
a 120-second total infrastructure wall, a 16 MiB scientific payload, finite
serialization, and exactly 17 scheduled points per section.

No wall is action latency. No call count is a full-width forecast.
