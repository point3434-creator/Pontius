## What failed and what did not run

ADR-0352 made explicit total-tape enumeration a conservative small-game
mechanism. At every exact fan point or open-cell witness, it formed the
Cartesian product of all exactly maximizing actions across the responder's
information sets. It intended to re-evaluate every completion under its own
fixed tape, retain distinct total tapes even when their affine rows were
identical, and only then construct the exact maximum envelope.

The bound fired while computing that Cartesian cardinality. Consequently the
failed invocation serialized none of the following target evidence:

- no completed tie-aware section;
- no active tape, affine row, or affine equivalence class;
- no v2-versus-envelope adapter decision;
- no scheduled fixed-tape Float64 comparison;
- no source/current total or pruned-tape identity;
- no maximum-envelope or `z >= row` differential; and
- no gate vector beyond the outer typed rejection.

The Fraction oracle, v2 source-separation rule, envelope direction, and
total-versus-reachable semantics are not falsified by this terminal. Their
synthetic controls remain valid mechanism tests. They simply did not complete
the frozen h4 integration invocation.

## Structural diagnosis

The bound is doing useful work. A total behavioral tape assigns an action at
every information set, including information sets unreachable under a
particular upstream choice or zero-probability opponent branch. Exact ties at
several such information sets multiply into a large total-function strategy
set even when many completions are behaviorally indistinguishable at that
sample or induce the same affine row along the chosen section. Literal tape
enumeration makes those semantic distinctions explicit, but it pays their
full Cartesian cost before learning whether they collapse downstream.

The result does not prove that unreachable choices caused this particular
overflow; the artifact lacks the per-information-set trace required for that
attribution. It does establish that a fixed count of materialized tapes is the
wrong scaling variable for the next mechanism. Merely increasing 256 would
move the numerical coincidence and leave the representation family intact.

The structural successor must preserve, rather than erase, the distinctions:

1. represent the total-function active choice set as a factorized exact object
   with an arbitrary-precision cardinality and stable identity;
2. derive reachable-support identities as a separate reporting projection,
   never as certificate authority;
3. quotient only the affine rows, carrying exact membership/cardinality for
   every equivalence class instead of electing one representative as truth;
4. prove the compact maximum envelope equals exhaustive total-tape enumeration
   wherever exhaustive enumeration is feasible; and
5. fail closed before any bound on distinct affine rows or compact states is
   exceeded.

This is a representation change, not a relaxation of tie semantics. An exact
tie remains unresolved. Total identity remains conservative certificate
authority. The proposed quotient may compress equal mathematical rows, but it
may not pretend their distinct total tapes do not exist.
