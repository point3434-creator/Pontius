## Point/ray composition and complete schema

The normal fan remains the ray instrument. It locates every exact cell and
facet exposed along the section, including a response row strictly suboptimal
at the source that crosses later. At every fan boundary and open-cell witness,
the directional-face oracle independently obtains the complete active factor
set and its two slope extrema. The seam requires equal exact gain values,
matching total and reachable tie states, an affine row covering every open
cell, and strictly separated slope extrema at every selector-changing facet.

Every composed and scheduled face serializes:

- exact response value, profile utility, and deviation gain;
- minimum and maximum response slopes, the separate profile slope, and the
  resulting gain-slope interval;
- independent minimum- and maximum-slope total tapes;
- arbitrary-precision total-function and reachable-support cardinalities;
- every information set's actions, parent sequence, exact maximizers, positive
  counterfactual-support flag, local extremal actions/slopes, and state count;
- a digest of that factorized identity; and
- the complete logical-work ledger, including exact cardinality bit lengths
  and the materialized-tape count.

The result also retains complete fan cells, segments, boundary points,
three-valued total/reachable states, measures, tie points, affine gain rows,
composed samples, crossing scales, schedule observations, and semantic
digests. Thus a later solver-free owner can rebind the evidence rather than
trust its outer hash or a pass Boolean.
