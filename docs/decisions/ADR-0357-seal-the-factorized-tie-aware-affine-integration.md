## Independent mathematical checks

The maximum of affine fixed-response gain rows is convex and piecewise affine.
Therefore its open-piece slopes are nondecreasing and adjacent pieces meet
exactly. At an interior facet, the active face's minimum and maximum slopes
are the left and right derivatives. At a domain endpoint, only the inward
one-sided derivative must own a fan cell, while the full active face remains a
valid point observation. The controls explicitly exercise both the interior
and endpoint cases.

An exhaustive small-game oracle enumerates every pure response only inside a
two-action synthetic control and matches the factorized envelope at five exact
rational scales. A 12-level chain exposes 4,096 total-function responses and
13 reachable-support responses with zero materialized tapes. A repeated-actor
control preserves four total functions versus three reachable behaviors.
Mutated cardinality, slope seam, envelope direction, source dispatch, call
ledger, and exact-window bound all fail closed.

These controls are verification of the committed finite implementation, not
certification for arbitrary games. The inherited 256 guard bounds discovered
fan pieces, the 100,000 guard bounds explicit tree nodes, and neither bounds
active-face cardinality.
