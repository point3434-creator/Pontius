## Claims boundary

ADR-0377 establishes a passing six-point scaling curve through 40 available
cards for the exact frozen quotient mechanism on the named workstation. It is
a spot-check of this device, runtime, populations, and recorded invocation,
not certification of all GPUs or future environments. There is no counterpart
here for 45 cards, a complete full-width leaf contraction, one CFR iteration,
one solve, a legal poker action, a 15-second decision, decision-quality gain,
support truncation, or poker strength. The absence of those results is a
boundary, not an unfavorable finding.
