## Integer-chip settlement boundary

The trace found a semantic seam that the synthetic target could not expose.
`StructuredShowdownAutomaton` divides a pot equally in Float64, while
`NoLimitBettingState.settle` awards integer shares and odd chips in button-
relative order. Those operators are not generally interchangeable.

This keystone therefore admits only one flat pot, equal sunk contributions,
all six live contenders, and a pot divisible by every possible winner count.
For six contenders that divisor is `lcm(1,2,3,4,5,6) = 60`; the frozen pot is
exactly 60. Under that precondition the legal integer settlement and the
automaton's fractional split are identical for every possible tie set. A
folded seat, unequal sunk contribution, second side pot, pot not divisible by
60, or target action that does not end in showdown rejects before the
automaton or quotient is used.

This is an intentionally narrow existence gate, not a claim that odd chips
or side pots are irrelevant. A future production leaf automaton must either
encode exact side-pot/odd-chip semantics or preserve this fail-closed scope.
No tolerance may stand in for chip identity.

## Seat and card coordinate contract

The contraction axes are logical storage axes, not table seats. The frozen
mapping is table seats `(1,2,3,0,4,5)` on logical axes `(0,1,2,3,4,5)`.
Logical axes 0, 1, and 2 are the fully summed source half. Logical axis 3 is
the controlled singleton and logical axes 4 and 5 are the other query-side
opponents. The target is therefore on the query half, exactly as ADR-0367
requires. Moving the target or any other open axis to the source half rejects.

The five public cards and controlled hand are removed before local quotient
coordinates are assigned. The remaining physical cards, in increasing deck
order, map bijectively to local cards 0 through 44. Every opponent axis is the
same complete `C(45,2) = 990` physical-hand domain in local-combination order.
The controlled hand is a singleton outside that local universe. Partial,
reordered, aliased, duplicated, or stale axes reject rather than being
silently remapped.

The full host fixture must have widths `(990,990,990,1,990,990)`, 8,145,060
source occupancies with 90 source pairings each, 148,995 query occupancies
with six query pairings each, 893,970 labeled query records, and 164,221
containment keys. Those are structural identities only. The source seal may
construct host metadata and the direct showdown automaton, but it must not
execute the full-width quotient operator or derive a solve/iteration wall.

## Reduced exact differential

The only numerical correctness gate is a frozen reduced view of the same
context. Local cards 0 through 9 remain. Each of the five opponent axes has
four explicitly frozen hands; all four hands on every axis occur in at least
one compatible deal. The five private axes have 1,024 Cartesian assignments,
six complete compatible assignments, 16 compatible source records collapsing
to 15 source occupancies, and six compatible query records with six query
occupancies. The hero remains the same fixed physical hand.

On this view the source seal must prove conjunctively:

1. every public action and terminal state comes from the legal betting kernel;
2. the structured automaton equals exact legal integer-chip settlement for
   every compatible assignment with zero chip error;
3. exact `Fraction` quotient forward rows equal literal card compatibility;
4. the labeled transpose and its exact dot-product identity hold;
5. folded per-hand numerator, reach, and conditional value agree within
   `2e-11` with the existing non-dense open-mode showdown consumer;
6. the same fields agree within `2e-11` with
   `contract_heterogeneous_leaf_terms`, the terminal consumer called by the
   leaf-adjoint evaluator; and
7. a simultaneous source-seat/table-axis permutation leaves the semantic
   result unchanged while an unpaired relabeling is detected.

The reduced values are control evidence only. They are not a strategy label,
menu comparison, full-width estimate, quality result, or population sample.

## Preparation, residency, and warm ownership

The bridge must report three disjoint typed ledgers.

- **Preparation-only work** covers physical/local card mapping, pair-to-hand
  construction, source/query pairing templates, labeled query generation,
  and strength-code compilation.
- **Resident state** covers the automaton runtime tables, mixture and unary
  weights, mode factors, pair lookup, pairing template, query masks/indices,
  and axis offsets actually required by a later quotient consumer.
- **Warm-mutable state** is limited to unary weights and terminal path mode
  factors. Topology, card mapping, query labels, seat mapping, and automaton
  semantics may not mutate under a warm update.

Byte counts and logical operation counts are allowed. Timings are reporting-
only if collected and may not be compared with ADR-0307's 15-second action
wall. No preparation work may be renamed resident or warm merely to improve a
future capacity ledger.

## Adversarial controls and kill criteria

Reject the source seal if any config/dependency digest differs; if the exact
context cannot be replayed from the authoritative betting kernel; if the
belief has other than 20 bound likelihood updates or a stale card/policy
digest; if any opponent axis is not the complete 990-hand domain; if physical
and local card coordinates are not bijective; if the target or another open
axis is sourced; if check does not produce a six-way showdown; or if flat-pot,
equal-contribution, one-pot, or divisor-60 settlement preconditions fail.

Reject also on any legal-settlement, literal-forward, exact-transpose,
dot-product, open-mode, heterogeneous leaf-adjoint, source-permutation, warm-
ownership, count, dtype, contiguity, immutability, or provenance failure. Any
CuPy import, device call, full-width forward/adjoint value, target-owner call,
action emission, quality label, latency gate, truncation selection, or
blueprint training inside this source boundary kills it rather than widening
the claim.
