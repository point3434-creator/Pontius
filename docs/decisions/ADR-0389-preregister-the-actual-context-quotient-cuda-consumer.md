## Offset-aware CUDA operator

The consumer uses one physical Float64 workspace of stride 128 for two logical
global ranges, `[0,128)` and `[128,176)`. Active widths are 128 and 48; state
features are global 0 through 174; reach is global 175 and belongs only to the
second slice. Every source, signed-target, fold, and covector kernel receives
the global feature start and active width. Source states and terminal states
are compared in global coordinates. Local column zero of slice 1 must never
masquerade as global state zero or global state 128.

The additive module must provide the ten named kernel roles in the config.
The old zero-based monolithic source/fold kernels are forbidden because their
semantics are insufficient at a nonzero feature start. Inactive columns are
never active work and are poisoned in bounded controls. Logical work is
exactly 176 columns, not 256; the 48-column range is an active view, not a
second physical workspace.

Forward streams 893,970 labeled query records in fourteen chunks of at most
65,536, ending with 42,002 records. Each chunk is signed, folded, reduced, and
consumed before reuse. Fold accumulates an unnormalized numerator and reach;
there is exactly one normalization after both slices. Slice-local conditional
values are forbidden.

Adjoint query work streams 148,995 complete four-card occupancies in fourteen
chunks of at most 10,922 occupancies, or 65,532 complete labels, ending with
7,009 occupancies and 42,054 labels. A chunk may not split or omit a six-label
group. Source output streams 8,145,060 six-card occupancies in 249 chunks of
at most 32,768, ending with 18,596. The source adjoint is a fused contraction
of exact source-pairing weights with the unique adjoint; it may not materialize
a full source-coefficient matrix or a 90-fold expanded source adjoint.

The query covector is the derivative of the unnormalized numerator. State
entries are query weight times terminal winner value; reach entries are query
weight times sunk net-chip value. Both forward-fold and transpose therefore
return net chips times unnormalized compatible mass. Reach is unnormalized
compatible mass, and their final ratio is conditional net chips. NashConv,
strategy quality, and systems throughput are not these units.

## Determinism and exact work ledger

Every feature slice executes twice and must return byte-identical outputs.
Floating result accumulation uses fixed increasing chunk order and a fixed
pairwise in-place reduction tree; atomics, `cupy.sum`, `einsum`, `repeat`,
advanced-indexing products, and unnamed numeric temporaries are forbidden.
Minimizing launches may not merge semantic ownership classes.

The two Float64 accumulator slots have a typed reuse protocol. In forward they
hold numerator and reach. Each slice snapshots the pre-slice bytes to host,
executes once, captures, restores the pre-slice state, executes again, requires
byte identity, and retains exactly one contribution; slice 0 cannot change
reach. After both slices, the final forward pair is copied and synchronized to
host before both device slots are positive-zeroed. In adjoint, slot 0 holds the
transpose dot while slot 1 must remain positive zero; the same snapshot,
restore, repeat, and single-contribution rule applies. Forward and adjoint
results therefore never require extra co-resident device storage and repeats
cannot be double-counted. The eight host scalar meanings and their order are
frozen explicitly in the config, ending with the total transpose identity.

Work quantities carry their semantic unit rather than sharing a generic
"full-pass" label. One slice performs 733,055,400 source-pairing visits,
14,303,520 signed-query vector terms, 744,975 label vector additions,
464,268,420 signed-source vector terms, and 733,055,400 source-adjoint pairing
visits. The complete 128+48 partition therefore performs 1,466,110,800 source
pairing visits, 28,607,040 signed-query vector terms, 1,489,950 label vector
additions, 928,536,840 signed-source vector terms, and 1,466,110,800
source-adjoint pairing visits.

Scalar work for that complete partition is independently frozen as
9,789,072,480 source-recurrence additions; 2,517,419,520 signed-query
additions; 156,444,750 fold-state visits; 131,115,600 label additions;
112,741,200 adjoint-recurrence additions; and 81,711,241,920 signed-source
additions. The two-repeat execution doubles each complete-partition count; the
config records those totals explicitly. A one-slice vector term and a
complete-partition scalar addition may never share a field or denominator.
The reverse family has exactly 57 terms, never 63
(`sum(C(6,k), k=0..4)=57`).

Across two slices and two repeats, forward has four source builds, 24
recurrence-level launches, and 56 signed/fold/reduce/accumulate chunks.
Adjoint has 56 query covector and label-aggregation chunks, 16 recurrence-level
launches, and 996 signed-source/contract/reduce/accumulate chunks. Counters are
semantic work evidence and must not be converted into latency.

## Pre-seal diagnostics and corrections

Fresh review changed the prospective contract before its hash was sealed. The
sample ranks were already deterministic and unchanged, but their label family
was implicit; the config now freezes the exact template and all six lane
labels. The two device result slots and eight host scalar rows had correct byte
capacity but underspecified reuse semantics; the config now freezes snapshot,
restore, repeat, positive-zero, forward-to-adjoint handoff, and scalar order.

The most important correction was semantic, not numerical. Several proposed
work numbers were arithmetically correct but called "per full feature pass"
even though one was a one-slice vector-term count and its neighbor was a
complete-128+48 scalar-addition count. Those fields were deleted. The final
config separately names one-slice vector work, complete-partition work, and
the frozen two-repeat execution, and independently derives every total. No
kernel, population, allocation, numerical ceiling, runtime, artifact, or
claim was changed because none yet existed or had run.

The first combined status/documentation invocation reported three harness/
prose failures: one historical ROADMAP continuity spelling, one backtick-
sensitive phrase check, and an ADR-0376 integrity test that compared today's
append-only artifact-marker digest with the marker bytes sealed by ADR-0376. The
first two were wording only. The third was a real test-design smell: it now
binds ADR-0376's historical digest explicitly while separately checking the
current additive marker contents. The corrected 89-test group passes. This
does not amend ADR-0376 or relax any historical artifact authority.
