## Decision

Yes, at CPU source scope only. Accept the source seal and stop before choosing
a fixed-width representation or opening any new numerical population.

The source encodes each captured `(high, low)` pair as its exact dyadic sum
under one family-wide exponent. It then evaluates both differently scaled
factorial recurrences with integer additions, signed integer weights, and no
internal division. The forward numerator, adjoint numerator, and 720 times the
literal disjointness bilinear form are identical integers. Forward reach equals
720 times literal reach and must be positive. Source and common scale cancel
from the conditional ratio; one integer-only round-to-nearest-ties-to-even
conversion emits the sole binary64 answer.

The bound contract keeps semantic quantities separate. Source/covector/weight
entry maxima produce independent forward tables, adjoint tables, signed
partials, products, numerator, and reach bounds. Required signed bits are
derived from each bound, then one explicit guard limb is added. Under the
legacy diagnostic envelope, the source seal reproduces 245/241 table bits and
480/473 numerator/reach bits: five guard-inclusive table limbs and nine
guard-inclusive scalar limbs. No ten-card exponent measurement is promoted to

## Algebra and population controls

The complete natural ten-card population reconstructs current paired capture
semantics rather than converting the older exact-original-factor authority.
Each of 210 six-card rows sums all 90 labeled pairings in the frozen order;
each of 1,260 query records captures its paired weight and all 176 paired
covector features. Independent `Fraction.from_float(high) +
Fraction.from_float(low)` literal contraction matches the operator's exact
conditional value, and the one rounded binary64 output matches by bits.

The complete signed twelve-card population supplies nonzero low components,
opposite signs, signed cancellations, zeros, and wide exponent alignment over
924 source rows and 2,970 labeled query rows. Forward, adjoint, and literal
integer numerators match; every scaled output is divisible by 720; reach is
positive; observed partials remain inside their semantic bounds; source,
record, label, and feature permutations preserve the exact integers.

An additional pre-seal audit found and repaired one semantic-count defect: the
first source represented query labels only as “six records per occupancy.” Six
duplicates could satisfy that numerical count while semantic labels were
missing. `CapturedOperatorInput` now carries explicit label integers and every
occupancy must contain `{0,1,2,3,4,5}` exactly once. Missing-label and duplicate-
label controls both reject. This is the structural repair; no recorded result
used the weaker predicate.

Direct induction controls compare every forward level with
`(6-k)!` times its literal containment marginal and every adjoint level with
`(4-k)!` times its literal marginal. Copied forward weights, wrong scale,
reversed signs, dropped lows, incomplete labels, out-of-window exponents,
nonfinite components, nonpositive reach, one-limb-short allocations, overflow,
and delta epoch changes reject or are detected as frozen.

The terminal converter passes half-even, half-odd, binade carry, half-minimum-
subnormal, above-half subnormal, minimum-normal seam, negative-zero underflow,
exact zero, and overflow controls, then matches Python's independent exact
Fraction-to-binary64 conversion by bits on 500 deterministic signed cases.

## Next-boundary hypotheses, not authority

The next preregistration should compare mechanisms rather than assume one:

1. Treat the transform as a selective oracle. After building `H`, one selected
   source adjoint uses 57 subset terms; after building `G`, one selected query
   uses 16. Dense adjoint output remains available only where a claim requires
   every coordinate.
2. Let Float64 propose warm iterations only if the final authority exactly
   re-evaluates the active basis and also proves global separation/pricing or a
   conservative bound over every omitted row and column. Active-basis replay
   alone is insufficient.
3. Compare positional limbs with residue channels under the same unbounded-
   integer authority. RNS must freeze primes and reductions, prove product of
   moduli exceeds twice every signed bound, retain divisibility semantics, and
   prove any redundant-channel detection claim under an explicit error/range
   model. The scaled operator has no reason to divide by 720 internally.
4. Compare resident rank/card/adjacency tables with row-owned traversal that
   unranks once per row and derives child colex ranks incrementally. Do not call
   the latter constant-time until its exact rank proof and work ledger pass.
5. Record both reporting-only stored-component `frexp` extrema and authoritative
   canonical odd-mantissa exponent extrema separately for source, covector, and
   weight families in every future population event.

Each is a hypothesis for a preregistered work, memory, correctness, and device
gate. None is selected by ADR-0435.
