## Positional result

The candidate uses signed little-endian two's-complement 64-bit words. Addition,
subtraction, negation, signed small-constant multiplication, and full signed
schoolbook multiplication execute limbwise. High products, carries, borrows,
sign extraction, and sign restoration are retained. Every named result checks
its semantic absolute bound and requires the sole extra limb to be exact sign
extension.

The frozen-window source-seal derivation reproduces 211 forward-table/partial
bits, 207 adjoint-table/partial bits, 405 numerator bits, and 381 reach bits.
The corresponding guard-inclusive allocations are 5, 5, 8, and 7 limbs.
Random differentials, long carry/borrow, non-sign guards, one-limb-short plans,
mathematical-minimum rejection, cancellation, high-half products, product-
window failure, and both complete operator populations pass or reject as
frozen.

## RRNS result and narrow theorem

All nine frozen moduli pass deterministic unsigned-64 Miller--Rabin, small-
prime, pairwise-coprimality, scale-coprimality, ordering, and redundant-largest
checks. The four-working table product is 248 bits; the eight-working scalar
product is 496 bits. Their exact integer products satisfy the matching
`M >= 2B + 1` inequalities. Every modulus's Montgomery constants, conversions,
zero/one/boundary operands, and `(p-1)*(p-1)` product match unbounded modulo
arithmetic without a truncated product.

Both full-codeword CRT/range and working-CRT/base-extension checks reconstruct
every valid boundary value. Every changed residue in every one of the nine
channels is detected at zero, signed values, and both bound edges. An exhaustive
small code at equality `M = 2B + 1` tests every legitimate integer and every
alternative residue in every channel. A consistent wrong in-bound integer
encoded across all channels passes RRNS and fails only the unbounded
differential, proving the correlated-fault boundary. Two-channel observations
are explicitly ineligible for the single-channel claim. Detection never
becomes correction or retry.

All residues remain scaled by 720. Reconstruction precedes divisibility, a
nondivisible reconstruction rejects, and structural mutations that give a
config or source operation the semantic role of inverting 720 or one of its
factors reject. Resident-nine and batched-five-then-four schedules produce the
same exact operator integers; the latter records the second four recurrence
replays.

## Operator, access, rank, and telemetry results

The comparison begins at captured binary64 high/low pairs, not after a hidden
unbounded aggregation seam. Each family is exactly fixed-point encoded under
its own frozen canonical-exponent window. Labeled query rows and weights are
then aggregated through limb additions or independently through every RRNS
channel before either recurrence begins. The aggregates, all forward and
adjoint rows, both numerator directions, reach, and terminal binary64 bits
match ADR-0435 on complete 10 and signed 12.

That aggregation boundary was found during the pre-seal audit. The first draft
had recomputed recurrence and scalar work through each candidate but inherited
ADR-0435's already-aggregated query rows. No retained result used that narrower
comparison. Moving aggregation inside both candidate paths is the structural
repair and adds an R199 control against future hidden unbounded preprocessing.

After `G` exists, each selected forward coordinate reads exactly 16 subset
rows; after `H` exists, each selected adjoint coordinate reads exactly 57.
Every reduced coordinate matches its dense parent output. The global scanner
accepts iterables, binds the complete cardinality and order, retains no output
rows or price dictionary, and rejects an active-basis subset, one omitted
coordinate, reordered input, and a positive exact reduced cost. This is a
global-scan primitive, not a complete resolver certificate.

Row-owned colex traversal unranks once, advances insertion position at most
the parent width, and derives each child from one binomial lookup plus two rank
adds. Exhaustive n=10/n=12 controls and first/middle/last literal-45 rank-only
parents pass. Lexicographic substitution, off-by-one rank, wrong insertion,
and duplicate-child mutations reject.

High and low components are reported separately for source, covector, and
weight families. Combined reporting-only `frexp` and authoritative canonical
odd-mantissa extrema reproduce every frozen complete-10 and signed-12 count.
Each family admits both exact window edges and rejects one exponent beyond;
positive zero, negative zero, subnormal, sign, all-zero, and nonfinite cases
remain typed. No observed telemetry sizes the source-seal bounds.

heterogeneous total, throughput conversion, latency, or fit verdict.

The pre-seal audit corrected two accounting errors. Batched RRNS initially
charged nine-channel modular arithmetic but only five-channel load/store
traffic; it now charges both batches, repeated capture reads, and four-channel
recurrence replay. One memory field initially combined forward and adjoint
second-batch scratch; it now reports both scratch populations and both replay
inputs separately. Forward reach products and Montgomery low/high products are
also explicit. These corrections changed no operator value and selected no
candidate.
