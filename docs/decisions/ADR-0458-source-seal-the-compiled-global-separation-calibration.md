- The next one-shot owner has a complete, independently readable source chain
  and cannot silently move scientific authority onto the host.
- The rejected resident-nine physics cannot return under new buffer names; the
  sole five-channel arena makes concurrent residency unrepresentable.

  work, timing, or evidence gates without producing a production decision.

- The source is deliberately large because it contains the literal CUDA
  translation unit, all four mechanisms, both arithmetic schedules, and the
  verification surface in one sealed dependency.
- Reduced source-only memory and work proofs do not establish target fit.
- The complete calibration remains expensive and its first invocation may end
  at an infrastructure or resource terminal before any timing comparison.
- Even a passing calibration cannot select a production topology until a real
  base producer and certificate-frequency contract exist.
