## Question

Slice C of v0a increment one ("origins/imports/inventory/profile/CI") ran
rounds r008, r009 and r010 and then entered a partial core replacement of the
helper-authority state inside `tools/generate_test_inventory.py`, with an R1/R2
checkpoint ladder, two mutually blind cold reviews and a CPU acceptance wall
still ahead of it. The r010 pair
`29c02f6fbd5eb0b7ddc9e816ef28f570b9839358` /
`8741fa20b1ebc7e9e9d226f463680c603c348f1332bf19c8ae12b29579f76deb`
is rejected NOT CLEAN, with four Important findings reproducing sixteen public
false negatives on both interpreter slots. Meanwhile the increment's product
slices A and B are accepted CLEAN/SOUND twice and preserved byte-identical at
r007.

Does sealing increment one require the analyzer to be sound, or only that the
increment's clone-safe suite subset joins the continuous-integration wall?

## Decision

**The analyzer repair leaves increment-one acceptance scope and continues as
its own bounded task.** ADR-0485's permitted-paths constraints require exactly
four things of this machinery: register `tests/test_v0a_hand_replay.py` in the
inventory declaration, regenerate inventory and profile bytes through the
authorized writer, refresh only mechanically affected census expectations while
granting no capability, and add the CPU clone-safe suite to CI without demoting
a hard gate. Criterion 9's required evidence is "New direct CPU gate and
unchanged existing hard gates." The generator appears in the increment brief's
seam inventory, not its acceptance criteria. Nothing in the preregistration
conditions increment-one acceptance on the analyzer's capability inference.

**Increment one's slice C obligation is therefore registration only:** add the
four v0a test paths to `STABILIZATION_TEST_FILES`, regenerate the inventory and
profile bytes, refresh the two mechanically affected expectation constants in
`tests/test_inventory_and_profiles.py` (the `stabilization_test_files` tuple and
the unresolved-dynamic-blocker count 355 to 356), record the one conservative
blocker named below, and add the v0a origins and the CPU gate under criteria 9
and 10 unchanged.

**This decision accepts, in writing, that increment one registers over a
generator with known unsound capability inference.** That is justified on this
evidence and not in general: the increment grants no capability, its seam
inventory admits no subprocess, GPU or network, and the analyzer's only response
to v0a code is a refusal rather than an approval. The r010 defect class is false
negatives — unsafe outcomes wrongly approved — and this registration produces no
approvals for that class to corrupt.

**The rejected r010 pair and the R1/R2 rewrite artifacts are retained
unchanged as evidence.** Parking is not deletion and not acceptance. R1 Gate A
(source `c8fc013d2be7599aa41b873552dd2a11191f2be1342d1a8f2bd04a39dc1dbd5f`) and
the closed R2 checkpoint 1
(`41b4de563da886a7c674d49b25acd4332ba208906b403ec244d1a4aea856ee05`) remain
frozen and immutable; the in-flight checkpoint 2 stops where it is. The
successor task gets a fresh brief carrying the four Stage 0 fields installed
below, and must state whether a hand-built abstract interpreter of Python
semantics is the right shape for a test capability inventory at all, or whether
declaration or observation removes the defect class.

**The Gate 13 freeze lane stays paused at revision 23.** Its predeclared
classifier kill criterion fired on a sixth residual; the disposition is recorded
at `tmp/freeze-tools-design-r003-draft/classifier-shape-disposition-2026-09-04.md`
(SHA-256 fff9a91a9a7f5e6bcc7d19ed3db1cde4148f1cbb8da10c64fcf55683ec125f5a).
No revision 24 is authorized, no full rewrite is authorized, and no sixth proof
tier is authorized. The successor direction is an oracle-backed differential
corpus replacing the hand-enumerated family-completeness claim.

## Housekeeping absorbed by this decision

1. **Stage 0 ground-truth discipline (installed).** `docs/workflow.md` Stage 0
   now requires four written fields in every brief: ground truth (where expected
   behaviour comes from, with "the author's own enumeration" a permitted answer
   that must be stated), dependency statement (what waits; everything unnamed
   proceeds), stop rule, and budget. The brief template carries matching fields.
   A check whose oracle came from the same assumption as the implementation
   cannot test that assumption; six Gate 13 residuals and the r009/r010 pair
   were all found externally, never by their own apparatus.
2. **Proportionality (installed).** `CLAUDE.md` gains a proportionality section:
   a support component larger than the component it protects needs written
   justification before it grows further; prove observable behaviour and bind
   implementation structure only where a named threat requires it; a proof an
   attacker could defeat by editing the proof is defending nothing.
3. **Line-ending hazard (open).** Git reports that LF will be replaced by CRLF
   on `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/workflow.md` the next time it touches them. The
   repository requires LF-only governance bytes. A `.gitattributes` rule pinning
   governance paths is owed before this corrupts a byte-exact artifact.
4. **Backup obligations carried from 2026-08-29 (open).** The restore drill,
   push automation, and the retained-evidence inventory test remain open. The
   snapshot branches `backup/2026-08-29-main` and
   `backup/2026-08-29-evidence-stab` remain live and are retirable once the
   content they guard is committed.
5. **Project skills (open).** The `.claude/` scaffolds `/preseal-checklist` and
   `/new-lane`, which would bake learned defenses into successor lanes, remain
   unwritten.
6. **Freeze-tools operator documentation (deferred with the lane).** The
   controller-requested operator documentation for the freeze utility is
   deferred while the lane is paused, and returns with the successor design.

## Kill criteria

The parked analyzer task returns under a fresh brief with a named independent
oracle. If its successor produces a second residual on the same contract, the
custom abstract-interpreter shape is abandoned rather than repaired again, and
the capability inventory moves to declaration or observation. If the Gate 13
successor admits one reference-rendered block start that reaches ordinary text
under one independent review, the custom Markdown classifier is abandoned in
favour of an authoring validator plus a frozen manifest, whose manifest must
carry the cross-document relations and not only paths and digests.

## Claims boundary

No claim is made that the capability inventory is sound; it is known unsound and
registered over deliberately, for one increment, on the stated evidence. No
claim is made that increment one is accepted, sealed, rehearsed or measured. No
claim is made about poker strength, action timing, or any decision-quality
result. The diagnosis behind this decision is a diagnosis, not acceptance
evidence: the registration it exercised was performed inside a throwaway
snapshot, and the real registration is a reviewed edit under the increment's own
Tier-C sequence.
